This one's a genuinely easy yes, which makes the question itself a bit of a tell: people mostly ask it because they're not sure the brand is actually Australian in the first place, and want to check it's not some overseas-only thing before they commit to a shirt built on it.

It isn't overseas-only, but it also isn't Australian at origin — it's a New Zealand company, founded in Auckland, that happens to have expanded hard into Australia and now operates plenty of stores and warehousing on this side of the Tasman. "Available here" and "from here" are two different questions, and this one's easy: available here, definitely. From here: no, but close enough geographically that supply, stock and turnaround aren't the problem they'd be sourcing a blank from the other side of the planet.

That distinction matters more than it sounds like it should, because it's the whole reason the blank is usable for a small AU brand in the first place. A tee that's genuinely stocked and warehoused nearby means real turnaround times, not a six-week wait behind an international shipment — which is the actual, practical thing "available in Australia" is standing in for when someone types that question into a search bar.

That's also worth knowing if you're the one shopping for blanks yourself, not just buying finished tees: local availability means samples, exchanges and re-orders happen on a timeline measured in days, not the weeks you'd be looking at sourcing a blank from further afield. For a small brand still working out sizing and fit, that's not a minor convenience — it's the difference between fixing a mistake quickly and being stuck with a bad batch for a month while a replacement ships.
