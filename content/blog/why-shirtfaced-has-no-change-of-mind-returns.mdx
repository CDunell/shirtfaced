Plenty of retailers advertise "no questions asked" returns as a selling point. We don't, and the reason isn't that we're trying to dodge responsibility for anything we actually got wrong — it's that "no questions asked" and "we stand behind what we make" are two different promises, and only one of them is honest to make without qualifying it.

The real, firm line: faulty, damaged, wrong item sent, or not as described — always sorted, in line with Australian Consumer Law, no argument. That's not a policy we're generous enough to offer; it's a legal obligation every retailer in this country already has, whether they publicise it or not. What's genuinely optional, and what we've chosen not to offer, is a return purely because someone got the shirt home and decided the vibe wasn't quite right.

The reasoning is mostly practical, not philosophical. A returned tee that's been worn, even once, can't ethically go back into stock and get sold to someone else as new — it isn't new anymore. That leaves two real options: eat the cost of restocking something that can't actually be resold, which gets priced into every single sale to cover the ones that come back that way, or price things honestly for people who bought thoughtfully in the first place and let a size guide do the job of preventing regret before it happens rather than refunding it after.

We picked the second one. It means the size guide, the fabric weight, the fit description all need to be genuinely accurate and worth reading properly before you buy — not filler copy nobody's expected to check — because there's no safety net behind a bad decision made without reading it. That's a real trade we're asking you to make, not a hidden catch.

It also means the boundary is drawn in a specific, deliberate place: your decision about whether you like it isn't refundable, but our mistake always is. Faulty print, wrong size sent, garment that arrived damaged — none of that is remotely your problem to absorb, and none of it falls anywhere near the change-of-mind line. Reading the size guide properly before ordering the [Mentally On Annual Leave Tee](/products/mentally-on-annual-leave-tee) or the [Spin Cycle Tee](/products/spin-cycle-tee) is the actual, practical version of the safety net — not a refund after the fact for a decision that was always yours to make carefully the first time.
