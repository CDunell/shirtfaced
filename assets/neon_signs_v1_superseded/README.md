Flat, print-ready word-in-a-box designs — rounded rectangle outline with the
word inside, neon-tube register. Transparent background (RGBA, verified via
alpha channel), not on white. Delivered 2026-08-11, 13 words x 4 colourways
= 52 files, organized here by colourway.

bad, beers, champion, decisions, dickhead, dramatic, fuck_off, good_times,
great_mates, itsbullshitmate, mates, nice_one, yeah_nah

## Colourways

- `amber/` — amber/orange neon glow
- `lime/` — lime green neon glow (matches the smiley mark's green)
- `white/` — white neon glow
- `black/` — flat black outline, no glow — the printable single-colour ink
  version, not a colourway of the glow effect

## raw_textures/
