# Stock packs

Bought or downloaded artwork, kept whole and unconverted, with the agreement it
came with beside it.

Kept apart from `assets/` proper because these are sources rather than elements.
Nothing here is loaded by the archive: the converter reads SVG and raster, and
these arrive as `.ai` and `.eps`, which it cannot open yet. That is a converter
for us to write, not a reason to leave the files out — and holding the pack now
means the work can start whenever the converter exists.

| Pack | Formats | Agreement |
|---|---|---|
| `vectornet/` | `.ai`, `.eps`, `.jpg` | `USER-AGREEMENT.pdf`, kept verbatim |
