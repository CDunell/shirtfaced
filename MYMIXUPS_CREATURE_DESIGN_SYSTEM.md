# MY MIXUPS — CREATURE DESIGN SYSTEM

This file is the authority for generating and developing My Mixups creature artwork.

## Core idea

My Mixups are impossible animal hybrids that behave visually as if they are completely ordinary animals. The humour comes from the anatomy itself. The creature must not visually explain, acknowledge or exaggerate the fact that it is a hybrid.

## Visual system

- Hand-drawn, slightly imperfect soft-charcoal outline.
- Flat to subtly mottled/weathered colour; never glossy or polished digital rendering.
- Restrained internal detail.
- Small/simple eyes and neutral, deadpan expressions.
- Slightly awkward anatomy.
- Mostly side-on presentation.
- Compact, garment-print-friendly proportions.
- Strong readable silhouette at thumbnail size.
- No scenery, props, shadows, decorative marks or environmental elements.
- Development boards use a warm bone/cream background.
- Development boards contain no names, numbers, captions or other typography.
- Do not drift into slick children's-book illustration, postcard Australiana or cute-cartoon exaggeration.

## Hybrid construction

Build each creature from two, occasionally three, recognisable animal characteristics, but do not simply attach Animal A's head to Animal B's body.

Distribute incompatible traits throughout the animal: body mass, face/snout/beak, legs/feet, ears, tail, fins, shell, spines, fur, feathers, antennae and proportions.

The result must read as one animal. There are no Frankenstein seams, stitching, dividing lines or deliberate transition markers. Anatomically ridiculous; visually believable.

The creature itself does not notice anything is wrong.

## Colour rule — critical

Colour must NEVER explain the hybrid.

Do not colour-code the donor animals or make colour boundaries routinely coincide with anatomical mixup boundaries. A dominant colour should often continue straight across an impossible anatomical join.

Secondary colours may occur as believable natural variation: belly, muzzle, extremities, dorsal area, feather variation, underside or irregular patches. These colour regions can and should cross hybrid boundaries.

Use roughly 2–4 muted colours per creature, including neutral/bone areas. Some creatures should be nearly monochromatic; others may use three distinct muted tones. Do not default every creature to the same distribution.

### Palette anchors

- Dusty eucalyptus — `#78877A`
- Muted sage — `#92977D`
- Faded ochre — `#B28A50`
- Soft clay — `#A96752`
- Dusty denim — `#687C84`
- Weathered plum — `#806F77`
- Warm bone — `#D8CBB5`
- Soft charcoal — `#343532`

These are reference anchors, not eight mandatory exact swatches. Nearby dusty/weathered variations are encouraged.

Never use acid lime, clean primaries, rainbow palettes, high saturation or glossy colour.

## Silhouette diversity

Each new collection must deliberately vary body architecture. Across a board include a broad mix such as squat/heavy, round, low/elongated, high-backed, front-heavy, rear-heavy, big-headed, tiny-headed, long-legged, short-legged, feathered, smooth, shaggy, spined, shelled, finned, long-tailed, almost tailless, long-snouted, blunt-faced, beaked and antennaed forms.

Do not allow a board to collapse into repeated four-legged potato silhouettes.

## Novelty rule — critical

Every creature on every previously accepted development board is unavailable for future boards unless explicitly requested for further development.

A new creature cannot be:

- an exact repeat;
- a recolour;
- a mirrored repeat;
- the same body with different ears/tail;
- a slightly longer/shorter version;
- the same obvious hybrid combination;
- a substantially equivalent silhouette.

The drawing language and colour system repeat. The creatures do not.

## Production constraints

- Avoid excessive horizontal width because of POD printable areas.
- Avoid extremely thin appendages that disappear in print.
- Avoid huge empty spaces between body parts.
- Prefer solid readable silhouettes.
- Wider creatures may be carefully shortened/fattened or made more compact, but never flattened into roadkill.
- Preserve recognisable character and intentional proportions.

## Development board standard

Default exploration board: **25 completely new creatures in a 5 × 5 grid**.

- Comparable visual mass between slots.
- Clear negative space around every creature for later extraction.
- Warm bone/cream background.
- No names.
- No numbers.
- No captions.
- No palette legend unless explicitly requested.
- No repeated or near-repeated creatures from previous boards.

## Selection and master development

Development boards are concept exploration only. Once a creature is selected, preserve its identity and develop that specific creature rather than regenerating a vaguely similar substitute. Final artwork should retain the selected silhouette, anatomy and character while being cleaned only as necessary for production.

## One-line generation authority

> Generate 25 entirely new deadpan animal Mixups in the established hand-drawn My Mixups visual system, using dusty muted natural colouring that treats each impossible hybrid as one completely ordinary animal, while repeating neither creatures nor near-equivalent silhouettes from any previous board.
