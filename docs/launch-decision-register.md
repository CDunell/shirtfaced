# SHIRTFACED — Launch Decision Register

Status: Living document  
Started: 5 August 2026  
Scope: Zero to first audience  

## Purpose

This document records the decisions that matter before SHIRTFACED has followers, products or customers.

It is limited to:

- account setup
- launch sequencing
- discovery
- content rollout
- paid testing
- measurement
- launch operations

It does not cover later-stage brand building, community programs, customer retention, collection strategy or scaling.

## Evidence standard

Every recommendation must be labelled as one of:

- **Platform guidance** — explicitly supported by Meta, Instagram or TikTok.
- **Industry evidence** — supported by credible launch examples, operator experience or multiple converging sources.
- **SHIRTFACED hypothesis** — a deliberate test where reliable evidence does not exist.

Unsupported internet conventions are not treated as facts.

# 1. Corrections to the first draft

The original execution plan contained several unsupported assumptions. These are withdrawn.

## 1.1 Private build before launch

**Withdrawn claim:** Keep the Instagram account private until launch.

**Correction:** Instagram recommendation eligibility applies to public accounts. A professional account should be public and checked through Account Status before discovery testing.

**Current classification:** Platform guidance.

## 1.2 Preloading 9–12 posts

**Withdrawn claim:** Launch with 9–12 posts.

**Correction:** Meta provides no official launch-grid number. The requirement is to publish enough content for a visitor to understand the account. The exact number remains a SHIRTFACED experiment.

**Current classification:** SHIRTFACED hypothesis.

## 1.3 Delaying all paid promotion

**Withdrawn claim:** Do not use paid discovery until organic winners emerge.

**Correction:** Broad paid spending without a defined purpose is wasteful, but early controlled testing is valid. TikTok Spark Ads can amplify organic posts while retaining engagement on the original post.

**Current classification:** Platform guidance plus SHIRTFACED test design.

## 1.4 Instagram polished, TikTok behind the scenes

**Withdrawn claim:** Instagram should be polished while TikTok should be process and behind-the-scenes content.

**Correction:** SHIRTFACED is faceless and documentary-led. TikTok should carry the same world in a more immediate, motion-led, platform-native format. Behind-the-scenes content is optional, not mandatory.

**Current classification:** SHIRTFACED decision.

## 1.5 Fixed daily cadence

**Withdrawn claim:** One feed post and one Reel every day.

**Correction:** No fixed daily cadence has yet been justified. Cadence must be tested against production capacity, reach, watch behaviour and content quality.

**Current classification:** Open decision.

# 3. Account creation

## Decision 01 — Create accounts early or only at launch

### Question

Does creating Instagram and TikTok accounts weeks before posting provide any distribution advantage?

### Current evidence

No reliable platform evidence has been identified showing that account age alone creates an organic-distribution advantage.

### Current decision

Create accounts early for operational reasons only:

- secure handles
- configure security
- assign ownership
- complete business integrations
- prepare bios and links
- test permissions

Do not treat account age as an algorithmic tactic.

### Classification

SHIRTFACED operational decision.

---

## Decision 02 — Public status before rollout

### Current evidence

Instagram recommendations to non-followers require a public account that remains recommendation-eligible.

### Current decision

Instagram will be public before discovery testing begins. Account Status must be checked before publishing launch content.

### Unknown

There is no confirmed advantage in making the account public days or weeks before the first post.

### Classification

Platform guidance plus open timing decision.

---

## Decision 03 — Business or Creator account

### Current leaning

Use an Instagram Business account and a TikTok Business account.

### Reason

SHIRTFACED is a commercial brand, not an individual creator identity. Business accounts better align with:

- advertising
- future commerce integrations
- shared administration
- business analytics
- organisational ownership

### Remaining check

Confirm whether any music-library restrictions materially affect SHIRTFACED's planned content before locking the Instagram account type.

### Classification

Platform-aligned infrastructure decision, pending restriction review.

# 4. Ownership and governance

## Decision 04 — Business ownership of digital assets

No critical launch asset should be owned only through one founder's personal account.

Assets requiring documented ownership and recovery access:

- domain
- business email
- Instagram
- Facebook Page
- Meta Business Portfolio
- Meta ad account
- TikTok Business Account
- TikTok Business Center
- website
- analytics
- payment provider
- design and production tools

### Operating rule

Each critical asset must have:

- a named primary owner
- a second authorised administrator where supported
- two-factor authentication
- recovery details
- access recorded in the internal asset register

### Classification

SHIRTFACED governance decision.

# 5. Tracking before traffic

## Decision 05 — Install measurement before public rollout

Before the first discovery campaign or meaningful traffic:

- configure website analytics
- configure Meta Pixel where applicable
- configure TikTok Pixel where applicable
- define conversion events
- establish UTM naming rules
- confirm source attribution
- test events

### Reason

Traffic that arrives before measurement is installed cannot be reliably reconstructed later.

### Classification

Operational best practice; implementation details remain to be verified against the final website stack.

# 6. First content and profile depth

## Decision 06 — What gets posted first

### Unknowns

No platform guidance establishes a universal first-post format.

Open options:

- documentary still
- Reel
- carousel
- simultaneous initial set

### Current direction

The first published content must establish the SHIRTFACED documentary world. It must not be:

- a mission statement
- a founder introduction
- a "coming soon" tile
- a logo-only post
- an explanation of the brand

### Classification

SHIRTFACED decision.

---

## Decision 07 — Preload content before active discovery

### Evidence position

There is no official Meta rule requiring a nine-tile or twelve-tile launch grid.

### Real decision

How much profile depth is needed before sending strangers to the account?

### Test design

The initial profile must contain enough content that a visitor can:

- recognise the visual world
- understand the account without an explanatory post
- view more than one format
- decide whether to follow

The exact quantity will be set after the initial content inventory is known.

### Classification

SHIRTFACED hypothesis.

# 7. Discovery from zero followers

## Decision 08 — Instagram discovery tools

### Current evidence

Instagram Trial Reels can show content to non-followers first, allowing creators to test discovery without relying on an existing audience.

### Current decision

Trial Reels will be included in the Instagram launch test plan where available on the account.

### Required checks

- confirm feature availability
- confirm recommendation eligibility
- record non-follower reach
- compare Trial Reels with standard Reels

### Classification

Platform guidance.

---

## Decision 09 — TikTok discovery approach

TikTok will not be treated as a duplicate Instagram feed.

The same documentary world will be adapted into:

- stronger motion openings
- faster entry into the scene
- native vertical sequencing
- platform-appropriate sound where legally usable
- multiple edits from the same source material

It will not default to founder-led or behind-the-scenes content merely because generic advice says it should.

### Classification

SHIRTFACED platform execution decision.

# 8. Paid discovery

## Decision 10 — Paid activity at launch

### Rejected binary

The choice is not simply:

- paid immediately, or
- no paid until much later.

### Current rule

Do not run broad awareness spending merely to make the account look active.

Small paid tests may begin early when all of the following exist:

- a defined audience hypothesis
- a clear creative hypothesis
- working measurement
- a spending cap
- a pass/fail criterion

### TikTok

Use Spark Ads where appropriate so paid engagement accumulates on an organic post.

### Instagram

Paid testing rules remain open pending Meta-specific launch research.

### Classification

Platform guidance plus SHIRTFACED test design.

# 9. Posting cadence

## Decision 11 — Days 1–30 cadence

### Current evidence position

No universal posting number has been established for SHIRTFACED.

TikTok-published business material has reported average organic posting rates among business accounts, but those observations are not universal rules and do not establish the correct cadence for this brand.

### Current operating principle

Cadence must satisfy all four conditions:

- enough volume to generate useful learning
- enough quality to preserve the visual world
- enough runway to avoid exhausting the library
- enough consistency to compare results

### Decision status

Open.

### Required input

The cadence will be chosen after counting:

- launch-ready stills
- launch-ready videos
- variations per scene
- weekly production capacity

# 10. Measurement

## Decision 12 — What counts as success before followers exist

Follower count is not the primary early metric.

### Instagram launch metrics

- non-follower reach
- profile visits
- follows from profile visits
- shares
- saves
- Reel watch behaviour

### TikTok launch metrics

- views
- average watch time
- completion rate
- rewatches where available
- shares
- profile visits
- follows from profile visits

### Website launch metrics

- sessions by source
- landing-page engagement
- email sign-ups, if email capture exists
- product-interest actions, if products are not yet available

### Classification

Measurement framework; exact thresholds remain open.

# 11. Review and failure protocol

## Decision 13 — Review cadence

Do not react to every weak post by immediately changing the entire strategy.

### Current direction

Use structured review windows rather than emotional daily changes.

Candidate rhythm:

- daily operational check for errors
- twice-weekly creative review
- weekly decision review

### Not yet final

The review rhythm will be adjusted to match publishing volume and paid-platform learning requirements.

---

## Decision 14 — Failure protocol

Before launch, define what happens when content underperforms.

Possible variables to change one at a time:

- opening frame
- edit length
- caption
- cover image
- publishing time
- format
- audience target
- paid support

### Rule

Do not change multiple major variables simultaneously when the purpose is to learn what caused the result.

### Classification

SHIRTFACED testing discipline.
