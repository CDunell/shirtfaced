# shirtfaced — creative brain

**Status:** ACTIVE / SOURCE OF TRUTH FOR CREATIVE JUDGEMENT  
**Purpose:** Preserve the judgement, attitude and creative instincts discovered through repeated concepting, rejection and refinement. This is not another visual identity manual. It explains how to think when making shirtfaced.

---

## 1. The thing we are actually building

shirtfaced is a premium Australian streetwear brand with a persistent visual and cultural universe.

It is not funny-shirts merch. It is not pub merch. It is not surfwear, skate cosplay, Americana, tattoo art, Harley, generic grunge or Australiana souvenir shit.

Australian origin and attitude are enough. Do not add the tea towel treatment.

The identity is not a single illustration style, font treatment, photography recipe or joke format. The identity is the judgement behind the choices.

We can use photography, illustration, typography, brand-only graphics, deadpan captions, loud graphics, tiny observations, vintage references, contemporary treatments and deliberately stupid premises.

**Style is not the identity. Judgement is.**

**Different styles are allowed. Different personalities aren't.**

**Random incongruity isn't shirtfaced. Human truth + disproportion is.**

---

## 2. The central creative rule

> **The wearer gets it. Who gives a fuck if the audience doesn't? It makes the joke stronger.**

Do not explain.

Do not add a second line because somebody might miss the joke.

Do not turn an observation into a slogan plus explanatory copy.

Do not make the graphic intellectually self-contained for a stranger walking past.

The person wearing it bought the shirt. They are in on it. That is enough.

Mystery, confidence and restraint are stronger than explanation.

---

## 3. The personality

shirtfaced acts harder than it is.

It is sarcastic, inappropriate at exactly the wrong moment, loyal, affectionate, inclusive, irreverent and fundamentally warm underneath the bullshit.

It will bring you coffee every morning and answer your thank-you with:

**you're not welcome**

It will do anything for a mate and then give them shit for needing the help.

It swears like punctuation, especially when it probably should not, but swearing is not the product. A design does not become shirtfaced because FUCK is printed in 90-point type.

It is capable of genuine tenderness but would rather be caught dead than explain that tenderness on a shirt.

It knows when it is in the doghouse.

It will still push its luck.

It is the rare win immediately before fucking it up again.

---

## 4. Inclusion without theatre

The cultural position is simple:

**Come as you are. Don't be a dickhead.**

Everyone welcome. No dickheads.

No corporate inclusion theatre. No self-congratulatory messaging about being a decent human being.

People belong naturally inside the shirtfaced world. Their identity is not a campaign device.

Inclusive if you are invited. Barge your way in behaving like a dickhead and you are out.

## 5. Humour mechanics

shirtfaced humour is usually strongest when the delivery is completely straight.

The joke can live in:

- the mismatch between image and caption
- the seriousness with which something ridiculous is presented
- an observation that is wildly disproportionate to the subject
- a familiar social truth nobody needed written down
- somebody doing something they obviously should not do
- affection disguised as hostility
- confidence unsupported by evidence
- a tiny detail discovered after the main image lands
- a phrase that sounds like it escaped from an actual conversation

The joke should not arrive wearing a clown suit.

### A useful test

If removing the explanatory line makes the design funnier, remove the fucking line.

## 6. Canon examples

These examples teach the judgement. Do not mechanically reproduce them.

### Nun bombing into a motel pool

This is shirtfaced.

A nun in full habit, serene and completely committed, cannonballing into an ordinary motel pool while everyone else treats the moment seriously.

The energy underneath it is:

**You can't do that.**  
**Hold my fucking beer.**

The image carries the premise. It does not need an essay underneath.

### Old lady at a bus stop — LOOSE UNIT

Straight photography. Ordinary old lady. Nothing visually outrageous.

Caption:

**LOOSE UNIT**

The disproportion is the joke.

### Old bloke playing lawn bowls — ABSOLUTE WEAPON

Again: completely sincere photography. No comedy face. No visual wink.

Caption:

**ABSOLUTE WEAPON**

### Coffee — YOU'RE NOT WELCOME

Affection expressed as antagonism.

This is a core shirtfaced emotional mechanism, not merely a phrase.

### Lifeguard in a toddler high chair — BIG DOG

The modern toddler high chair is the concept. The lifeguard is utterly earnest. The visual contradiction carries the design.

### We Lost Gary

Five mates became four. Nobody is particularly concerned. The empty chair is the joke.

Do not explain where Gary went. Do not make people search for Gary. The night simply continued.

### Council workers / trench

Ordinary Australian documentary image. Two workers watching the third do the digging. Serious faces. Nothing performed for camera.

The observational treatment can elevate the ordinary without turning it into novelty merch.

### Heeler / Koolie rule

If a dog is used as a shirtfaced subject, default to a heeler or Koolie. It belongs to the actual world rather than functioning as a generic fashion mascot.

## 7. A canonical failure

### NOTHING SPECIAL. EVERYTHING CELEBRATED.

This sounded like a reasonable summary of the worldview.

That was exactly the problem.

It **explained** shirtfaced instead of **being** shirtfaced.

A brand manifesto printed on a shirt is not automatically a shirtfaced design.

The worldview should be inferred from the body of work.

This failure is important. Do not delete it from the institutional memory.

## 8. Photography

Photography should feel like evidence from a real world, not advertising pretending to be real.

Nobody poses unless the concept specifically demands portraiture.

Nobody deliberately presents the garment to camera.

People are doing something. The camera happened to be there.

Imperfection is useful: awkward crops, movement, available light, accidental framing, flash when appropriate, grain, mundane surroundings, ordinary people.

The strongest north star remains:

> **Every image should be good enough that you would share it on Instagram even if the shirt wasn't in it.**

The product is evidence of the world, not the reason the world exists.

WORLD 01 — THE BIG NIGHT remains canon: ordinary Australians, Friday night through sunrise, pubs, streets, servos, kick-ons, friendship, movement, optimism, bad decisions and available light.

Do not turn that into generic drunken-party photography. The warmth matters.

---

## 9. Australian without Australiana

The brand is Australian because of its people, humour, social codes, language, places and attitude.

It does not need koalas, maps, flags, meat pies, cork hats, faux pub signs or heritage cosplay to prove it.

Ordinary Australian reality beats Australian iconography.

Use the culture. Do not merchandise the stereotype.

---

## 10. Brand marks

The wordmark is always:

**shirtfaced**

Lowercase. Always.

Do not casually capitalise it in a logo treatment because a composition seems to want capitals.

The established shirtfaced smiley is an identity asset. **Do not redraw, reinterpret or improve it casually.** Use the actual asset.

The wordmark, smiley and established lockups are ingredients, not mandatory signatures on every design.

A shirt does not need to scream the brand name to belong to the brand.

## 11. Treatment vocabulary

The goal is a rotating stable of treatments large enough to create recognition without rinse-and-repeat sameness.

Valid lanes include:

- smiley alone
- lowercase wordmark alone
- wordmark + smiley lockup
- stacked lockups
- horizontal lockups
- outline type
- grey-on-black / tonal work
- tiny chest or incidental marks
- oversized back statements
- integrated type crossing or interacting with imagery
- caption beneath photography
- caption above photography
- type embedded inside the composition
- small observation against a large image
- loud phrase with minimal imagery
- image-only design
- text-only design
- image + text
- illustration
- straight photography
- distressed treatments when appropriate
- clean treatments when appropriate
- collegiate treatments when appropriate
- restrained premium brand treatments
- deliberately louder club/team-adjacent treatments

No single treatment is shirtfaced. The stable is shirtfaced.

Recognition should emerge from repeated instincts, marks, tone and judgement — not a template.

## 12. Black

Black is home base.

It is not a creative prison.

Black garments support white, grey, bone, acid/lime, selective colour and photographic/illustrative treatments. Tonal grey-on-black work is particularly useful when restraint makes the idea stronger.

Do not add colour merely to make a design look more designed.

---

## 13. Creative range

Change the subject.

Change the medium.

Change the decade.

Change the composition.

Change the volume.

Change the treatment.

Keep the judgement.

A run of successful photographic-caption designs does not mean the next ten should be photographic-caption designs.

A successful distressed graphic does not make distressing part of the constitution.

Do not confuse a hit with a formula.

**Different styles are allowed. Different personalities aren't.**

The brand can wear a thousand visual costumes. The bastard underneath must remain the same.

---

## 14. Concept, treatment, personality

Every design should be understood as three separate layers:

### Concept

The human truth, behaviour, relationship, social observation, stupidity or contradiction.

This is the reason the design exists.

A concept is not "Dutch master painting", "Japanese boxing poster", "1960s fashion illustration" or "heritage crest". Those are treatments.

### Treatment

The visual language used to disproportionately elevate, undermine or reframe the concept.

Treatment can change every fucking shirt.

Photography. Illustration. Heraldry. Editorial typography. Old sporting graphics. Fine art. Cheap flash. Embroidery. Screen print. Tonal minimalism. Loud poster work. Whatever serves the concept.

Treatment is allowed to be promiscuous.

It does not get to supply the personality.

### Personality

Always shirtfaced.

Personality does not move when the medium, era, reference or composition changes.

The treatment amplifies the concept. The personality determines whether the result belongs here at all.

**Concept → Treatment → Personality.**

Concept is the human truth.  
Treatment is how we fuck with its importance.  
Personality is shirtfaced and does not move.

---

## 15. Human truth + disproportion

**Random incongruity isn't shirtfaced. Human truth + disproportion is.**

Putting two unrelated things together can make an arresting image. That does not make it a shirtfaced idea.

The absurdity should have a recognisable human behaviour underneath it.

The old lady at the bus stop works because calling an entirely ordinary old lady **LOOSE UNIT** is affectionate social mischief.

The old bloke at lawn bowls works because **ABSOLUTE WEAPON** elevates ordinary competence into ludicrous mythology.

The nun bombing into the pool works because everybody understands the prohibition before she hits the water: **you can't do that**. Her commitment supplies the human truth. The visual disproportion does the rest.

The afterparty still life works because everybody recognises the pathetic archaeological site left behind the next morning. Rendering it with museum-level importance disproportionately elevates something mundane and familiar.

Surrealism alone is not enough. Weird alone is not enough. Unexpected alone is not enough.

Find the behaviour first.

## 16. The COSTUME TEST

shirtfaced can borrow almost any visual language.

Before approving it, remove the styling mentally.

If the underlying observation, behaviour, relationship or stupidity still feels shirtfaced, proceed.

If the entire appeal disappears when you remove "Japanese boxing poster", "Dutch master", "1960s fashion illustration", "heritage crest", "punk typography" or whatever treatment we are using, **we have designed a style, not a shirtfaced idea.**

The treatment amplifies the idea.  
It never supplies the personality.

**Different styles. Same bastard underneath.**

### Failure calibration — WORLD CHAMPION

An ordinary bloke exhausted on the edge of a bed, fighting to remove one impossibly stubborn boot as though he has survived twelve rounds: the underlying behaviour survives the test. That is a shirtfaced concept.

Rendering it as a fake Japanese boxing poster introduced borrowed personality. Remove that borrowed identity and keep the heroic disproportion. The concept survives; the costume gets killed.

### Failure calibration — BEAUTIFUL FORM

An elegant woman with a laundry basket on her head is not automatically shirtfaced. Without a recognisable behaviour underneath it, it is merely surreal fashion art.

Give the absurdity a human truth: she refuses to make two trips; both hands are occupied; she improvises and carries the washing with ridiculous dignity. Now there is behaviour underneath the image.

The fashion illustration can then elevate that behaviour. It is the treatment, not the idea.

## 17. The concept engine

The design archive and deterministic design engine will eventually operate over tens of thousands of garment/design references from hundreds of brands.

That means concepts should increasingly be separable into elements rather than welded to one arbitrary placement:

- subject
- human truth / behaviour / observation
- image/artwork
- phrase/observation
- brand mark
- treatment / visual language
- typography treatment
- composition relationship
- colour logic
- print treatment
- garment/application constraints
- personality conformance

Placement should ultimately be selected because it is the best fit for the concept and garment, informed by the design archive — not because every idea was born as a centred back print.

Concept first. Treatment second. Placement as a decision. Personality as the conformance layer.

The engine may recombine concepts and treatments. It must not recombine personalities.

---

## 18. What to reject aggressively

Reject work that is:

- technically competent but generic
- novelty-shirt humour
- explanatory
- safe streetwear imitation
- generic punk/grunge pasted over weak thinking
- Australiana shorthand
- random incongruity without human truth
- random vintage imagery with a random swear word
- borrowed visual language that supplies the entire personality
- faux-scientific or technical drawing language used because it looks designed
- diagrams, ID plates, botanical plates or engineering drawings without a genuinely exceptional reason
- mascot thinking disconnected from the actual shirtfaced world
- over-branded because the concept is weak
- visually busy because confidence is missing
- a repeat of yesterday's treatment with a new noun

The scientific/technical drawing lane has been explicitly rejected. Do not drift back into it because an archive reference makes it convenient.

---

## 19. Quality language

There are effectively three outcomes during concept development:

### FUCK YES

Immediate. Distinctive. The idea survives explanation because it never needed one. Worth developing.

### NEARLY THERE

The underlying concept is shirtfaced but execution, treatment, typography, composition or restraint is wrong. Fix the execution. Do not throw away the idea automatically.

### YEAH, I GUESS

Rejection.

Do not interpret politeness or reluctant acceptance as approval.

If the reaction is merely "okay", "I guess" or technically defensible silence, the work has not landed.

---

## 20. How to work with the creative director

Do the work rather than narrating what you could do.

When asked for concepts, produce concepts.

When asked for images, produce images rather than another page of copy.

When a direction is rejected, do not quietly circle back to it three rounds later.

Do not protect mediocre work with explanations.

Do not assume that because one style was approved it has become the required style.

Maintain continuity of judgement while actively seeking novelty of subject and execution.

The target is not agreement. The target is the **fuck yes**.

## 22. Before approving a concept

Ask:

1. What is the human truth, behaviour, relationship, social observation, stupidity or contradiction underneath this?
2. If I mentally remove the treatment, does the concept still feel shirtfaced?
3. Is the idea doing the work, or is the treatment supplying the personality?
4. Is this human truth + disproportion, or merely random incongruity?
5. Did we explain something that was funnier unexplained?
6. Would the wearer enjoy knowing something the audience might miss?
7. Is the humour delivered straight enough?
8. Does this feel like shirtfaced without needing the wordmark?
9. Is it Australian through lived reality rather than iconography?
10. Have we accidentally made novelty merch?
11. Are we repeating a treatment because it worked last time?
12. Is there one unnecessary element we can remove?
13. Is the reaction **fuck yes** or merely **yeah, I guess**?

If it is "yeah, I guess", keep working.
