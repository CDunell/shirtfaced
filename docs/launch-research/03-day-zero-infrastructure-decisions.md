# SHIRTFACED — Day 0 Infrastructure Decisions

Status: Working decision module  
Date: 5 August 2026  
Scope: Account type, music, links, email capture, Facebook and initial targeting

## Purpose

This module resolves the infrastructure decisions that must be made before SHIRTFACED begins public discovery.

It does not cover later-stage brand growth, retention, collection cadence or community building.

Every decision is classified as:

- **Platform guidance**
- **SHIRTFACED operating decision**
- **Open test**

---

# 1. Instagram account type

## Decision

Use an **Instagram Business account** from the beginning.

## Why

SHIRTFACED is a commercial brand with multiple founders, future paid media and a website. The account should be structured as a business asset, not as an individual creator identity.

## Operating consequences

The account must be:

- public before recommendation testing
- connected to the SHIRTFACED Facebook Page
- owned through the Meta business structure
- accessible to both founders through assigned permissions
- protected by two-factor authentication
- checked through Account Status before launch content is published

## Music constraint

Meta states that its licensed Instagram music library is intended for personal, non-commercial use. Certain business accounts and commercial posts may have restricted access. Meta provides Sound Collection as royalty-free audio that is safe for commercial use in Reels, Stories and ads.

## Final rule

Do not choose Creator merely to gain access to popular music.

SHIRTFACED is a business. Music must be selected from:

- Meta Sound Collection
- properly licensed original music
- commissioned music
- original recorded sound
- audio for which SHIRTFACED has documented commercial rights

Trending audio is not a foundation for the launch strategy.

## Classification

Platform guidance plus SHIRTFACED operating decision.

# 2. TikTok account structure

## Decision

Use a **TikTok Business Account** connected to **TikTok Business Center**.

Where practical, create or migrate toward an organisation-owned structure rather than leaving the account dependent on one founder's personal login.

## Why

TikTok Business Center supports:

- centralised asset ownership
- member permissions
- account and ad-account management
- access auditing
- team collaboration
- Spark Ads using the brand's existing posts

TikTok's organisation-owned account model is the strongest long-term control model where verification requirements can be met.

## Setup order

1. Create TikTok for Business login.
2. Create SHIRTFACED Business Center as an advertiser.
3. Confirm business name, country, timezone and currency.
4. Add both founders as administrators.
5. Create or connect the SHIRTFACED TikTok account.
6. Connect the advertiser account.
7. Complete business verification when legal documentation is ready.
8. Assign only the permissions each person needs.
9. Enable two-step verification.

## Classification

Platform guidance.

# 3. TikTok music restrictions

## Decision

Plan all SHIRTFACED TikTok content around the **Commercial Music Library**, licensed audio or original sound.

## Evidence position

TikTok states that Business Accounts are shown Commercial Music Library sounds when adding audio. The library is cleared for business use in organic and paid content. Businesses should not assume that the general consumer music library is commercially licensed.

## Final rule

The launch content system must still work when popular chart music is unavailable.

Each planned video must be able to use one of:

- Commercial Music Library track
- original location sound
- commissioned track
- licensed track
- voice-free sound design

Do not build an edit first and discover later that its entire timing depends on unusable music. That is how people create avoidable work and then blame the algorithm. Charming, but useless.

## Pre-launch test

Before finalising the first video batch:

1. Open the Commercial Music Library with Australia selected.
2. shortlist at least 20 suitable tracks
3. test availability in the TikTok app
4. record track names, duration and permitted placements
5. export alternate versions using original sound or neutral sound design

## Classification

Platform guidance plus production rule.

# 4. Facebook's role

## Decision

Create and complete a Facebook Page before Instagram launch, but do not treat Facebook as a primary organic content channel at zero stage.

## Purpose of the Page

The Facebook Page exists initially to support:

- Meta asset ownership
- Instagram connection
- advertising setup
- business identity
- future customer contact
- cross-platform permissions

## Launch behaviour

At Day 0:

- complete the Page name, profile image, cover image, username and website
- connect Instagram
- confirm Page roles and recovery
- do not spend launch effort trying to manufacture an active Facebook audience
- allow selected Instagram content to cross-post only when it displays correctly

## Final rule

Facebook is infrastructure first, distribution second.

Do not create a separate Facebook content plan before there is evidence that the audience is there.

## Classification

SHIRTFACED operating decision.

# 5. Link destination before products exist

## Decision

Do not send launch traffic to a dead ecommerce store, empty product collection or generic link hub.

Use one owned SHIRTFACED landing page.

## Required landing-page functions

The page must:

- identify SHIRTFACED immediately
- carry the documentary visual world
- explain almost nothing
- provide a clear single next action
- record traffic source through UTMs
- load quickly on mobile
- contain privacy and contact links

## Primary next action

Before products are available, the page's primary action should be:

**Get first access.**

Supporting copy can state that subscribers will receive the first drop or first release notice. Avoid fake scarcity, fake countdowns and vague “big things coming” sludge.

## Link rule

Use the owned domain as the profile link wherever the platform permits.

Do not use a third-party link hub unless multiple essential destinations exist. At zero stage, they do not.

## Classification

SHIRTFACED operating decision.

# 6. Email capture

## Decision

Install email capture before active discovery begins.

## Why

A follow is rented platform attention. An email address is an owned contact channel.

That does not justify blocking the website with a desperate full-screen popup before the visitor has seen anything.

## Capture design

Use:

- one inline signup on the launch landing page
- one footer signup
- optional exit-intent or delayed prompt only after testing

Do not use:

- immediate full-screen mobile takeover
- discount offer when no product exists
- fake VIP language
- daily-email promises
- pre-ticked consent

## Minimum fields

- email address

Optional later:

- first name
- location
- product interest

Do not ask for information SHIRTFACED has no immediate use for.

## Consent and data

The signup flow must record:

- timestamp
- source
- consent language shown
- landing page or campaign

Use a double opt-in only if required by the selected platform, risk preference or legal advice. The final implementation must comply with Australian spam and privacy requirements.

## Success measure

Track:

- landing-page sessions
- completed signups
- signup conversion rate
- source by platform and post

Do not judge the list by raw size without traffic context.

## Classification

SHIRTFACED operating decision; legal implementation requires confirmation.

# 7. Bio structure

## Instagram bio

The bio should contain:

1. what SHIRTFACED is
2. enough tone to distinguish it
3. the launch action

It should not contain:

- a founder story
- a mission paragraph
- five emojis
- “DM for collabs”
- “coming soon” as the entire proposition
- unsupported claims such as premium quality before a product exists

## TikTok bio

Use the same core identity, shortened for the platform.

Do not force identical wording if character limits or linking rules differ.

## Current working direction

**Australian streetwear. Friday nights documented properly. First access below.**

This remains copy to test, not immutable brand scripture.

## Classification

SHIRTFACED hypothesis.

# 8. Geographic targeting

## Organic launch

Do not geographically restrict organic content.

The world is explicitly Australian, but the platforms may find interested viewers outside Australia. Let them.

## Paid launch test

Initial paid discovery should target Australia only unless a specific test says otherwise.

## Initial paid geography

Start broad enough to let the platform learn, but not so broad that the test becomes meaningless.

Recommended first test structure:

- country: Australia
- age: legal adult audience aligned with the brand's intended market
- gender: all
- language: English where required
- location exclusions: none initially unless fulfilment or legal constraints require them
- interest targeting: broad or one deliberately defined hypothesis, not fifteen stacked interests

## Why

SHIRTFACED's documentary setting, language, cultural references and future fulfilment are Australian. The first paid test should determine whether Australians respond before buying cheap irrelevant reach elsewhere.

## Open test

Compare:

- broad Australian audience
- one streetwear/fashion interest audience
- one nightlife/culture audience, if platform targeting permits and the category remains appropriate

Keep creative constant across the comparison.

## Classification

SHIRTFACED paid-test hypothesis.
