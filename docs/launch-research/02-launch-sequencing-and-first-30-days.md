# SHIRTFACED — Launch Sequencing and First 30 Days

Status: Research-backed working decision  
Date: 5 August 2026  
Scope: Zero followers, zero customers, no public product launch required

## Evidence labels

- **Platform guidance** — directly supported by Meta or TikTok.
- **SHIRTFACED decision** — an operating choice based on the evidence and current constraints.
- **Experiment** — a controlled test where no reliable universal rule exists.

---

# 1. What the launch is actually trying to achieve

The first 30 days are not a conventional brand launch and are not a sales campaign.

The job is to:

1. make both accounts operational and discoverable
2. publish enough of WORLD 01 to make the account legible
3. create repeated opportunities for non-followers to encounter the work
4. identify which scenes, openings and edits earn attention
5. establish a repeatable publishing and review system before products or customers exist

Follower count is an outcome, not the operating objective.

---

# 2. Account state before the first post

## Instagram

### Platform guidance

Instagram only recommends content from public accounts. Professional accounts can check recommendation eligibility through Account Status. Professional accounts cannot be private.

Sources:

- https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/653964212890722
- https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/138925576505882

### SHIRTFACED decision

Before publishing:

- use a professional account
- keep it public
- complete username, profile image, bio and link
- connect the correct Facebook Page and Meta business assets
- enable two-factor authentication
- check Account Status
- confirm there are no recommendation restrictions caused by the bio, profile image or prior test content

Do not create a public account with placeholder branding or a half-finished bio. Account creation can happen early, but public discovery testing begins only when the profile is complete.

## TikTok

### SHIRTFACED decision

Before publishing:

- use the business-owned account
- connect it to TikTok Business Center
- complete profile, recovery and permissions
- confirm access to analytics and commercial-use audio
- prepare native vertical files rather than relying on Instagram exports

---

# 3. No artificial launch announcement

## SHIRTFACED decision

There will be no public countdown, founder introduction, mission statement, logo tile or generic “coming soon” post.

Reason:

There is no existing audience waiting for an announcement. Launch theatre performed to zero people wastes the strongest early profile positions and explains a world that should be understood visually.

The first visitor should encounter finished documentary evidence, not a notice that documentary evidence may arrive later.

---

# 4. Profile depth before active discovery

## Evidence position

Meta provides no official requirement to launch with nine, twelve or any other fixed number of grid posts.

## SHIRTFACED decision

Do not publish an arbitrary nine-tile dump.

Before deliberately driving discovery, Instagram must contain a **minimum viable profile**, defined by function rather than tile count:

- at least three distinct documentary moments
- at least one still or carousel
- at least one standard Reel visible on the profile
- no repeated scene disguised as separate ideas
- enough material to demonstrate that the account is a continuing world rather than one isolated image

### Initial operating number

Publish **four pieces across the first 24 hours**, not simultaneously:

1. documentary still or carousel
2. motion-led Reel from a different scene
3. documentary still from another point in the night
4. second Reel with a materially different opening and rhythm

This number is a SHIRTFACED launch experiment, not a platform rule.

### Why stagger them

Staggering allows:

- each post to receive a clean operational check
- captions, covers and account eligibility to be corrected before the next post
- early discovery signals to remain attributable
- the profile to gain depth within one day without looking like a bulk upload

# 5. Exact launch sequence

## Day minus 7 to Day minus 1 — operational setup

No public promotion.

Complete:

- account ownership and permissions
- two-factor authentication
- final profile image and bio
- link destination
- Account Status check
- analytics access
- file naming and publishing tracker
- captions and covers for the first seven days
- export validation on actual phones
- commercial music and audio checks

Prepare at least:

- 8 launch-ready Instagram assets
- 10 launch-ready TikTok edits
- 2 alternate openings for the strongest motion scenes
- 3 reserve posts that are not scheduled

The reserve prevents a broken export, rights issue or weak edit from forcing a bad post.

## Day 0 — account activation

### Morning

- final security and ownership check
- verify links
- check Instagram Account Status
- verify video playback, crop and cover display
- verify TikTok analytics access

### First publication

Publish one strong documentary still or carousel on Instagram.

Purpose:

- establish the visual standard
- give later Reel viewers a finished profile destination
- avoid making the account’s first visible object a format experiment

This is a SHIRTFACED decision, not a platform requirement.

### Two to four hours later

Publish the first Instagram Reel and first TikTok edit from the same source world, but not necessarily the identical cut.

Instagram version:

- supports the profile world
- deliberate cover
- no watermark
- caption consistent with the documentary voice

TikTok version:

- immediate motion or visual tension in the opening
- native vertical edit
- no copied Instagram UI or watermark
- sound chosen for TikTok, including silence where the scene benefits from it

TikTok’s fashion creative guidance explicitly treats aesthetic-only, still-photo, silence and disruptive formats as valid tests; it does not require creator monologues or behind-the-scenes footage.

Source:

- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/Fashion_2024/pc/en

### Evening

Publish the second Instagram still from a different scene in WORLD 01.

Do not ask friends to flood the post with generic comments. Do not buy engagement. Instagram states that misleading follower-building practices can affect account recommendation eligibility.

Source:

- https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/313829416281232

## Day 1

- publish the second Instagram Reel
- publish one or two TikTok edits, depending on available quality
- check only for operational failure: broken crop, muted audio, recommendation restriction, incorrect link or upload defect
- do not rewrite strategy from a few hours of data

## Day 2

Begin Instagram Trial Reel testing if the feature is available.

Trial Reels are shown to non-followers first and provide core engagement results after approximately 24 hours. Instagram can automatically share a successful trial to followers based on performance within the first 72 hours.

Source:

- https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/

## Days 3–7

Continue controlled publication while preserving comparison:

- Instagram: one primary publication per day, alternating still/carousel and Reel where inventory supports it
- TikTok: one to two materially different edits per day
- Trial Reels: no more than one active new test per day during the first week
- Stories: only when they add context or continuity; no obligation to manufacture daily filler for an audience that does not yet exist

This cadence is an initial experiment. It exists to create enough observations without burning the evidence library.

---

# 6. Instagram and TikTok are siblings, not duplicates

## Instagram job

Instagram is the persistent evidence archive.

It must:

- make WORLD 01 understandable from the profile
- preserve strong still photography
- use Reels for non-follower reach
- convert discovery into profile exploration and follows

## TikTok job

TikTok is the high-velocity edit-testing surface.

It must:

- test openings, pace, sequencing, sound and duration
- reuse source material without merely reposting Instagram files
- permit stranger, rougher or more immediate edits while staying inside the same documentary universe
- identify scenes that create watch behaviour before product selling begins

## Locked distinction

TikTok does not become a founder diary or fake behind-the-scenes channel. Instagram does not become a precious grid that refuses to test motion. Both carry the same world; each performs a different distribution job.

# 7. Discovery from zero

There is no audience to “warm up.” Discovery must be engineered through eligible public distribution surfaces.

## Instagram discovery stack

1. recommendation-eligible public account
2. standard Reels
3. Trial Reels where available
4. shares and direct-message circulation
5. Explore, feed recommendations, Search and Suggested Accounts
6. profile conversion after discovery

Instagram states that eligibility does not guarantee recommendation. It is the entry condition, not the result.

Source:

- https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/653964212890722

## TikTok discovery stack

1. native For You distribution
2. multiple creative edits from the same source scene
3. variation in opening, sequence, sound and duration
4. organic post analytics
5. Spark Ads only after a post or creative hypothesis justifies amplification

TikTok’s own fashion playbook says its creative observations are not rules and recommends testing a variety of creative ideas. That supports deliberate variation rather than one “perfect” launch edit.

Source:

- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/Fashion_2024/pc/en

# 8. Paid discovery in the first 30 days

## Day 0 rule

No broad paid awareness campaign on Day 0.

Reason:

At that point SHIRTFACED has no platform-specific creative evidence, no stable baseline and no need to manufacture vanity reach.

## Earliest paid-test gate

A small paid test may begin when all are true:

- tracking is confirmed
- the organic post is technically clean
- the test has one defined purpose
- audience and creative hypotheses are written down
- spend is capped
- the account has enough profile depth for a visitor to understand it

## Valid early purposes

- compare two defined audience hypotheses
- test whether a strong organic TikTok post gains useful profile visits through Spark Ads
- measure profile-visit or landing-page behaviour from a specific creative
- validate tracking before any future product campaign

## Invalid early purposes

- make the account look popular
- rescue a weak post
- buy followers
- compensate for an empty profile
- run an undifferentiated “brand awareness” campaign because launch advice says to advertise

## Initial decision

No paid activity before the end of Day 3.

The first paid decision review occurs after:

- at least four Instagram publications
- at least five TikTok publications
- at least one non-follower Instagram test
- a complete tracking check

This is a SHIRTFACED governance gate, not a universal platform rule.

# 9. First 30-day cadence

## Week 1 — establish a usable baseline

Instagram:

- 4 launch-day/first-24-hour publications
- then 1 primary publication per day through Day 7
- mix stills, carousels and Reels
- use Trial Reels where available

TikTok:

- 1–2 edits per day
- at least two materially different edits from the strongest scenes during the week

Review:

- operational checks daily
- no strategic conclusion before the first 72 hours except obvious eligibility or technical failure

## Week 2 — repeat and vary

Do not increase volume automatically.

Repeat the formats that produced useful attention while varying one major creative element at a time:

- opening frame
- first two seconds
- duration
- sound versus silence
- scene order
- caption or on-screen text treatment

The scene itself does not need to change every time; the distribution treatment does.

## Week 3 — identify repeatable evidence

A creative pattern becomes provisionally repeatable only after it performs across more than one post or scene.

One outlier is not a strategy.

Begin grouping results by:

- scene
- platform
- format
- opening type
- duration band
- sound treatment

## Week 4 — lock the next operating cycle

At Day 28–30, decide:

- sustainable Instagram cadence
- sustainable TikTok cadence
- which motion structures deserve continued production
- whether controlled paid testing produced useful evidence
- which content types should be stopped
- the minimum content-bank runway required for the next month

Do not transition into product-launch planning merely because 30 days elapsed. The next phase requires evidence that the publishing system works.

# 10. Measurement hierarchy

## Level 1 — eligibility and delivery

Instagram:

- Account Status clear
- non-follower reach exists
- Reel and Trial Reel distribution functioning

TikTok:

- posts receive For You distribution
- analytics records views and watch behaviour

If delivery is absent, diagnose eligibility, technical quality and account configuration before judging the creative world.

## Level 2 — attention

- average watch time
- completion behaviour
- rewatches where available
- shares
- saves on Instagram

## Level 3 — account interest

- profile visits
- profile-visit rate from reach or views
- follows generated from profile visits
- repeat viewers where available

## Level 4 — off-platform intent

Only relevant if a useful destination exists:

- link clicks
- landing-page sessions
- email sign-ups

No product conversion metric is required before products exist.

---

# 11. Decision thresholds without fake precision

The account has no baseline, so absolute performance thresholds would be invented.

For the first 30 days, use relative thresholds:

## Continue

Continue a creative structure when it beats the account median on at least two relevant measures, such as:

- watch time plus shares
- completion plus profile visits
- non-follower reach plus follows

## Retest

Retest when:

- attention is strong but profile interest is weak
- one platform responds and the other does not
- the scene performs but only one edit does
- technical or timing problems may have contaminated the result

## Stop

Stop a treatment after at least three clean tests when it repeatedly underperforms the account median and produces no compensating signal such as saves, shares or profile visits.

## Escalate to paid

Consider controlled amplification only when the creative already demonstrates a useful signal or the paid test is explicitly designed to answer a separate audience question.

# 13. Decisions locked by this document

1. No empty-account countdown campaign.
2. No mission statement, founder introduction or logo tile as launch content.
3. Instagram is public and recommendation-eligible before discovery testing.
4. Four staggered Instagram publications establish minimum profile depth in the first 24 hours.
5. Instagram and TikTok launch together but do not use identical exports by default.
6. TikTok remains documentary and faceless; it does not default to behind-the-scenes content.
7. Instagram Trial Reels are used where available.
8. No broad paid awareness on Day 0.
9. Earliest paid review is after Day 3 and only after the evidence gate is met.
10. First-month thresholds are relative to SHIRTFACED’s own emerging baseline, not generic benchmark numbers.
11. The first 30 days end with an operating-system decision, not an automatic move into product launch.
