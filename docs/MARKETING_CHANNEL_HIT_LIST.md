# SHIRTFACED — Marketing Channel Hit List

**Status:** Active channel strategy  
**Priority:** Execute after My Mixups launch system is moving  
**Source inspiration:** Steve Chou video — https://youtu.be/ZhSL5A_xEJI

## Objective

Build SHIRTFACED as an adult identity/culture brand where the content can entertain before it sells anything.

The core system is:

**Culture/content → TikTok/Shorts/Reels → creator amplification → drops → email/SMS → repeat buyer**

SHIRTFACED should not behave like a generic apparel advertiser. Product should exist naturally inside the world rather than every piece of creative announcing fabric weight, fit and a discount code.

---

## Priority order

### 1. TikTok — 10/10

This is the strongest early acquisition and brand-building fit.

Use WORLD content, especially THE BIG NIGHT, as entertainment first.

Creative territory:

- Pub.
- Taxi.
- Servo.
- Kebab.
- Bad decisions.
- Dawn.
- The morning after.
- Friends behaving badly but recognisably.
- Products appearing naturally inside the scene.

Avoid default fashion-ad language such as:

> Introducing our premium 240gsm cotton tee.

The content should still be watchable if the viewer never buys a shirt.

Use organic performance to identify winning hooks and scenes before putting meaningful paid spend behind them.

---

### 2. YouTube Shorts — 10/10

Repurpose the same vertical creative engine rather than creating a separate content operation.

SHIRTFACED’s documentary/phone-photo world is well suited to **10–30 second micro-stories**.

Distribution path:

**Vertical master → TikTok → YouTube Shorts → Instagram Reels**

Use Shorts to extend the life and discoverability of campaign-world material.

---

### 3. Instagram / Reels — 9/10

Instagram ranks higher for SHIRTFACED than for My Mixups because visual credibility and brand-world consistency matter heavily in adult streetwear.

Use the channel in two distinct ways:

#### Reels

- Reach.
- Short narrative clips.
- Drop teasers.
- Behind-the-scenes campaign material.
- Proven TikTok creative.

#### Feed / profile

- Brand legitimacy.
- Campaign photography.
- Product/drop presentation.
- Visual world-building.
- Consistent SHIRTFACED identity.

Do not make the profile look like a catalogue of isolated garment mockups.

---

### 4. Reddit — 8/10

Potentially a strong fit because SHIRTFACED can participate through humour, culture, design and Australian life rather than purely through product promotion.

Potential areas to research:

- Australian humour/culture.
- Streetwear.
- Graphic design.
- Independent brands.
- Music.
- Pubs/hospo where contextually appropriate.
- Fashion and fit discussions.

Use restraint. Reddit detects forced corporate participation almost immediately.

Organic participation comes before paid ads.

Potential value:

**Traffic + cultural credibility + discussion + search/AI mentions + later retargetable audiences**

---

### 5. Creator seeding — 8/10

Avoid generic fashion influencers as the default target.

Prioritise Australian creators who already inhabit a believable SHIRTFACED world:

- comedians
- musicians
- DJs
- photographers
- hospo personalities
- tattoo artists
- streetwear people
- culturally relevant creators with an actual point of view

The goal is not “influencer wearing sponsored shirt”.

The goal is:

> SHIRTFACED looks like it already belongs there.

Seed product, encourage natural use, and secure permission to reuse strong content in paid/social channels where possible.

---

### 6. Google Shopping + Performance Max — 6/10 initially

Useful, but lower priority than for My Mixups.

SHIRTFACED is initially more of a **demand-generation brand** than a pure demand-capture brand. Many of the strongest designs will not correspond neatly to high-intent generic searches.

Google becomes increasingly useful as:

- brand awareness increases
- branded search grows
- product catalogue expands
- remarketing pools grow
- category/search demand becomes clearer

Use initially for high-intent product/category capture and retargeting rather than expecting it to create the whole brand.

---

### 7. AI / GEO search — 6/10 now, stronger later

Establish the foundation early even though this is not the main launch engine.

The site and external footprint should clearly communicate:

- what SHIRTFACED is
- where it is based
- the design point of view
- garment/product quality
- drop structure
- shipping and fulfilment
- how it differs from generic novelty tees and generic streetwear

Potential discovery topics:

- Australian streetwear brands
- independent Australian streetwear
- graphic tees Australia
- alternative streetwear Australia
- Australian clothing brands similar to relevant reference brands
- independent graphic T-shirt brands Australia

Avoid generic SEO filler. Use strong editorial, campaign and product context.

---

### 8. Email / SMS retention

Build from day one and increase importance as drops and repeat buyers develop.

Key flows:

- Welcome.
- Abandoned cart.
- Drop announcement.
- Drop countdown.
- Back-in-stock.
- Post-purchase.
- Repeat-buyer early access.
- VIP/high-value customer segmentation.

Email and SMS should become a major owned channel around drops rather than relying on paid media to reacquire previous customers every time.

---

### 9. Direct mail — later

Not an acquisition priority before a meaningful repeat-customer base exists.

Potential future applications fit the brand well:

- Drop postcards.
- Posters.
- Stickers.
- Physical campaign ephemera.
- Repeat-buyer cards.
- Limited inserts tied to WORLD campaigns.

This can become particularly useful for retention and drop theatre once customer volume justifies segmentation and postage.

---

### 10. AppLovin — not yet

Ignore during early-stage growth.

Revisit only after SHIRTFACED has:

- proven conversion economics
- meaningful catalogue depth
- high creative throughput
- significant paid-media budget
- reliable attribution
- diminishing incremental returns on the core channels

It is a possible scale channel, not a launch requirement.

## Creative operating rule

Do not make separate “ad content” the centre of the system.

A WORLD shoot should be planned to create:

- hero campaign footage
- multiple 10–30 second narrative cuts
- TikTok hooks
- Shorts
- Reels
- campaign stills
- product-detail media
- retargeting creative
- email/SMS assets
- creator seeding material
- site/editorial assets

One production session should feed the entire channel stack.

---

## SHIRTFACED-specific guardrail

The product is part of the culture, not the punchline every time.

If a piece of content only works because it contains a call to action, discount, product spec or “shop now” line, it is probably too close to generic apparel advertising.

Build attention first, then let product and drops monetise that attention.

---

## Immediate working target

Once My Mixups priorities 1–5 are operational, turn SHIRTFACED priorities 1–5 into a practical launch/growth checklist covering:

- platform/account readiness
- WORLD 01 short-form content inventory
- first 30–50 vertical cuts
- creator target list
- organic posting cadence
- cross-platform reuse rules
- rights/reuse process for creator content
- retargeting setup
- first paid tests
- success/failure thresholds

Do not burn early effort on AppLovin or direct-mail acquisition before the core culture/content engine is working.
