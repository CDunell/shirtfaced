# SHIRTFACED — Graphic Design System Research

## Status
Active

## Purpose
Establish an evidence-based constitutional design language for SHIRTFACED. This document begins as the research brief and will be updated with findings only where supported by repeated evidence across successful premium graphic apparel brands.

## Scope
Research only. No concepts, slogans, collections, humour, or creative direction.

## Primary Questions
- Visual mechanics
- Layout archetypes
- Scale and spacing
- Typography systems
- Graphic composition
- Cohesion mechanisms

## Method
For each finding:
1. Observation
2. Evidence
3. Reasoning
4. Constitutional rule (if warranted)
5. Confidence
