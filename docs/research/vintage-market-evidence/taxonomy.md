# Vintage Market Evidence — Analysis Taxonomy

Use this vocabulary when visually classifying records. Do not infer a field when the listing photography does not support it.

## Tradition

`surf` · `skate` · `streetwear` · `australian-surf-street` · `hybrid`

## Graphic scale

`micro` · `small-chest` · `medium` · `large` · `jumbo` · `all-over`

## Placement

`left-chest` · `centre-chest` · `full-front` · `upper-back` · `full-back` · `sleeve` · `wrap` · `all-over` · `multi-placement`

## Composition mechanism

`single-object-hero` · `mascot` · `character-scene` · `illustrated-badge` · `institutional-mark` · `photo-plus-type` · `editorial-photo` · `event-identity` · `souvenir-graphic` · `repeated-mark` · `geometric-system` · `bordered-panel` · `type-led` · `small-front-large-back` · `asymmetric-hit` · `all-over-pattern`

## Illustration language

`clean-line` · `scratchboard` · `woodcut` · `cartoon` · `crude-drawing` · `airbrush` · `halftone` · `photographic` · `abstract` · `geometric` · `collage` · `none`

## Typography

`none` · `sans` · `serif` · `blackletter` · `script` · `hand-lettered` · `collegiate` · `condensed` · `warped` · `outlined` · `stacked` · `arched` · `mixed`

## Garment architecture

Record garment type, base colour, sleeve length, collar, stripe/panel construction, pocket, and whether the visual identity is carried by the garment itself rather than a discrete print.

## Analytical separation

Marketplace facts and visual interpretation are different layers. `sold`, price, title, listing ID and image count come from the source. Composition/style tags are our classification and may be revised after image review.

## Shirtfaced translation

Later analysis should extract **mechanisms**, never historical artwork. A useful mechanism should be expressible without naming the source graphic: e.g. `micro front identity + unrelated jumbo back illustration`, `one absurd object treated as serious emblem`, or `photograph interrupted by blunt institutional typography`.
