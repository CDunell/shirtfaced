why World 01 had to be a family outing rather than a night out.

## 1. Proposed — Anh

**Woman, 42. Gary's mate since before either of them had kids.**

Not family, not anybody's partner, not attached to the friend web through a
younger person. The one person who can give Gary shit and have it land, because
she has known him since he was nineteen and a father.

Where the others orbit Gary, she doesn't. That is the whole point of her.

**Fills:** a woman over 30 who exists in her own right; a peer for Gary; the
possibility of a night out that isn't a family outing.

**Occupation:** not proposed. Five of the existing fourteen have none and the
document is stronger for it.

## 2. Proposed — Josh

**Man, 29. Doesn't drink.**

Never explained. Never remarked on. Not in recovery, not religious, not on a
health kick as far as anyone in frame is concerned — he just doesn't, and it has
never once been a topic. Out as often as anybody, last to leave more often than
most.

**This one is asked for by canon.** `BRAND_VOICE.md` §1, the critical widening:
*"The sober mate, the pregnant mate, the driver are all still in the photo and
still shirtfaced. Nobody is excluded from their own night."* That is written down
as the thing that takes shirtfaced from *that many drinks* to *that level of good
time*, and there is currently nobody in the cast who demonstrates it.

He also solves a production problem. Vehicle Canon makes the car a prop nobody
enters or leaves, which leaves no reason for a car to be anywhere. A driver who is
never seen driving is exactly that reason.

**Fills:** the 29–37 hole; the male side of the young cohort; the single most
important piece of brand doctrine that has no person attached to it.

---

## 3. Proposed — Steph

**Woman, 33. Has a three-year-old.**

Out four or five times a year and treats each one accordingly. Arrives late having
sorted something out, is the most committed person there by eleven, and is the
first to say she's staying.

Nobody in the current cast has young children. Everybody is either childless or,
in Gary's case, done. In a real Australian friend group in this age range that is
the single most common life stage and its absence is conspicuous.

She is also the highest-value occasion generator in the cast: the hall pass, the
one night off, the late arrival, the mate who has to be home and isn't.

**Fills:** the 29–37 hole; the life-stage gap; a source of occasions that doesn't
depend on anyone behaving badly.

## 5. One note on the web, kept short

The fourteen existing names came from the owner's own family and friends, which is
exactly why they are good. It is also why the web is narrower than the country
it's set in.

`SHIRTFACED_CREATIVE_BRAIN.md` §9 says ordinary Australian reality beats Australian
iconography, and §4 says *"People belong naturally inside the shirtfaced world.
Their identity is not a campaign device."* Both point the same way: an ordinary
pub on an ordinary Friday in this country is not one background, and a cast that
is uniformly one thing is a subset of Australian reality rather than the whole of
it.

So one of the three above is proposed as not-another-Anglo-name, on authenticity
grounds rather than any other kind. The conditions that make it work are the
doctrine's own: it is never mentioned, never a device, never the reason a frame
exists, and nobody in a caption ever notices. If it is remarked on anywhere, it has
failed and should be cut.

The name itself is a placeholder. The point is the web, not the label.
