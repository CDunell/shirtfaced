# shirtfaced — Headwear Concept Library

Status: Working concept archive

Purpose: Explore caps, bucket hats, beanies and related headwear as product systems in their own right. Do not treat headwear as tees shrunk onto hats.

## Core cap concepts

H01 **lowercase** — Unstructured 6-panel. Tiny `shirtfaced` embroidered dead centre. Nothing else.

## Headwear signatures

Core: lowercase wordmark, sf, X-eye smile.
Attitude: GOOD SORT, TOO EASY, YEAH NAH, FUCK YEAH, OLD MATE.
Club: SFC, social club, individual sporting clubs.
Seasonal: racing cap, reversible bucket, legionnaire, pom-pom supporter beanie.

The X X cap is a priority prototype because it turns the smile into the physical product rather than merely placing a logo on it.
