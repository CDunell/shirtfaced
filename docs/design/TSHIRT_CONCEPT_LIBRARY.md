# shirtfaced — Garment Concept Library

Status: Working concept archive

Purpose: Build a broad selection of shirtfaced garment concepts before narrowing into production designs. Deliberately vary subject, visual language, amount of text, composition, garment type and execution while remaining unmistakably shirtfaced.

Core principle: dead-serious execution + ridiculous premise. The artwork should carry the idea wherever possible; words reward the second look rather than rescue a weak image.

## Hard guardrails

- `shirtfaced` is always lowercase in customer-facing use.
- Australia is origin, attitude, language and point of view — never tea-towel Australiana.
- Native wildlife, national foods, suburban infrastructure and other obvious Australian shorthand are not concepts by themselves.
- Scientific plates, technical drawings, blueprints, exploded views, engineering diagrams, numbered callouts and pseudo-academic diagrams are retired as a design lane.
- A concept must have real garment desirability. Clever is not enough.
- Crops, tanks, crews and hoodies are designed as their own garments, not resized tees.
- Punch up, not down.
- Keep subject matter wide: people, fashion, sex, relationships, sport, nightlife, music, work, travel, animals, objects, ego, confidence, failure, success, weirdness and beauty.

## Selection rule

The archive is intentionally not a collection yet. Repetition of treatment should be resisted. Future rounds should mix text-only, image-only and combined designs; photography, illustration, typography, badges, editorial layouts, crude graphics, polished graphics, contemporary streetwear and unexpected visual languages.

One theme connects all of it: **shirtfaced** — unapologetically Australian in origin and attitude, bold, funny, irreverent, willing to give anything a go, attracted to the absurd, happy to poke at stereotypes, and punching up rather than down.
