# SHIRTFACED — T-Shirt Concept Library

Status: Working concept archive

Purpose: Build a broad selection of Shirtfaced garment concepts before narrowing into production designs. The library should deliberately vary subject, visual language, amount of text, composition and execution while remaining unmistakably Shirtfaced.

Core principle: dead-serious execution + ridiculous premise. The artwork should carry the idea wherever possible; words reward the second look rather than rescue a weak image.

## Selection note

The archive is intentionally not a collection yet. Repetition of treatment should be resisted. Future rounds should mix text-only, image-only and combined designs; photography, illustration, typography, badges, editorial layouts, crude graphics, polished engraving, contemporary streetwear and unexpected visual languages. Subjects should move freely across Australian social life rather than becoming a catalogue of beer, tradies or stereotypes.

One theme connects all of it: **Shirtfaced** — unapologetically Australian in attitude, bold, funny, irreverent, willing to give anything a go, attracted to the absurd, happy to poke at stereotypes, and punching up rather than down.
