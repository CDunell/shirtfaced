# shirtfaced — Brand Garment Concept Library

Status: Working concept archive

Purpose: Explore garments where the brand itself is the subject, from subtle core pieces through casual, premium, loud and sporting-club territory.

## Permanent rules

- `shirtfaced` is always lowercase in customer-facing use.
- SFC/SNC/SCC/SBC may remain uppercase as monograms/initialisms.
- Brand garments must become desirable before brand recognition is strong enough to carry them on name alone.
- The long-term aim is for the brand mark itself to become enough.

## System note

`shirtfaced social club` is a garment architecture, not necessarily a legal sub-brand. It gives the range crests, numbers, supporter tees, member shirts, premiership drops, caps, polos, jerseys and group customisation while staying aligned with the core idea that the unit is the group.
