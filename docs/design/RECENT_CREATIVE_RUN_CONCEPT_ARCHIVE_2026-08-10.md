# shirtfaced — recent creative run concept archive

**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Status:** Working creative evidence / concept recovery  
**Purpose:** Preserve concepts generated during the creative calibration run that were not yet present as a committed concept set. Keep strong ideas, near-misses and rejects because the judgement around them is useful to the design engine and future creative sessions.

This document supplements `TSHIRT_CONCEPT_LIBRARY.md`, `BRAND_GARMENT_CONCEPT_LIBRARY.md`, `HEADWEAR_CONCEPT_LIBRARY.md` and `SHIRTFACED_CREATIVE_BRAIN.md`.

## Status language

- **STRONG / DEVELOP** — concept is shirtfaced; execution still free to change.
- **RETAIN / RECALIBRATE** — useful underlying concept; current treatment or framing is wrong.
- **REJECT EXECUTION / KEEP LEARNING** — do not reproduce the execution; preserve why it failed.

## Set A — wearer-first concepts

### A01 — YOU'RE NOT WELCOME
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** Genuine affection expressed as deliberate antagonism. Someone does something caring; gratitude is answered with `you're not welcome`.  
**Human truth:** shirtfaced acts hard around tenderness. The affection is real; the hostility is theatre.  
**Treatment:** Open. Deadpan, restrained typography is one option, but the concept must not become a generic slogan tee.  
**Rule:** Do not explain the relationship or add sentimental copy.

### A02 — COME AS YOU ARE. DON'T BE A DICKHEAD.
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** Inclusion without self-congratulation or corporate theatre.  
**Human truth:** Everyone is welcome until they behave like a dickhead.  
**Treatment:** Open: pure type, institutional sign language, premium restrained brand treatment, club language.  
**Rule:** Do not turn it into a manifesto about inclusivity. The line is enough.

### A03 — ABSOLUTE WEAPON — ordinary-person lane
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** Straight, sincere portrait/documentary photography of somebody spectacularly unlikely to warrant the description `ABSOLUTE WEAPON`.  
**Canon subject:** ordinary old bloke at lawn bowls.  
**Human truth:** affectionate Australian overstatement; disproportion creates the joke.  
**Treatment:** Documentary photography + minimal observation.  
**Note:** Distinct from earlier object-based `ABSOLUTE WEAPON` concepts. This human lane is the stronger shirtfaced calibration.

### A04 — HOLD MY BEER
**Status:** RETAIN / RECALIBRATE  
**Concept:** Somebody is already halfway through an objectively ridiculous commitment. The energy is `you can't do that` / `watch me`.  
**Canon benchmark:** nun cannonballing into a motel pool.  
**Human truth:** confidence, commitment and refusal to be sensible.  
**Treatment:** The words are optional and usually secondary. The image should carry the act.  
**Rule:** Do not repeat the nun mechanically; preserve the behaviour.

### A05 — RARE WIN
**Status:** RETAIN / RECALIBRATE  
**Concept:** Treat a magnificently trivial achievement with wildly disproportionate championship importance.  
**Human truth:** self-deprecating celebration of the occasional small victory.  
**Treatment:** Championship/victory language is available, but the specific treatment must serve the chosen event.  
**Known failure:** `NOTHING SPECIAL. EVERYTHING CELEBRATED.` explained the worldview and weakened the joke.  
**Rule:** `RARE WIN` can stand alone. No commentary explaining why the achievement matters.

### A06 — EVERYONE WELCOME. NO DICKHEADS.
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** A club/worldview identity based on simple behavioural membership rather than status.  
**Human truth:** warmth with a boundary.  
**Treatment:** Club/organisation identity, crest, embroidery, understated back mark, or pure typography.  
**Rule:** Do not invent lore to justify it. Do not make faux-pub Australiana.

## Set B — mixed-medium six

### B01 — THE KIDS TABLE
**Status:** RETAIN / RECALIBRATE  
**Concept:** Six fully grown adults crammed around a tiny children's party table, behaving completely normally and seriously.  
**Human truth:** adults accept ridiculous practical arrangements when the social situation demands it; nobody needs to acknowledge the absurdity.  
**Treatment:** Documentary/flash photography. Prefer image-only.  
**Known failure:** An execution pushed everyone into performing a `wild party`, making the joke obvious and losing the deadpan.  
**Rule:** The children's furniture is the joke. Nobody winks at camera. Nobody performs comedy.

### B02 — MUM SAID NO
**Status:** RETAIN / RECALIBRATE  
**Concept:** Ferocious, beautifully rendered panther mid-pounce wearing one pathetic little party hat.  
**Human truth:** rebellion reduced by the one authority that still apparently counts.  
**Treatment:** Premium illustration; tiny refined `MUM SAID NO` if text is used.  
**Risk:** Random incongruity. Must retain enough recognisable attitude/behaviour that the party hat is not merely surreal decoration.

### B03 — NICE ONE DICKHEAD
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** Affectionate insult delivered through a split hierarchy.  
**Treatment:** Front: tiny tasteful `NICE ONE`. Back: enormous `DICKHEAD` across the shoulders in sporting surname lettering.  
**Human truth:** praise and abuse arrive in the same sentence between people who know each other.  
**Rule:** No image and no explanatory copy required.

### B04 — THE PEACOCK
**Status:** RETAIN / RECALIBRATE  
**Concept:** Magnificent full-display peacock; feather eyes subtly replaced with the established shirtfaced X-eye motif.  
**Treatment:** Premium image-only illustration. No wordmark or caption required.  
**Rule:** Use the actual locked smiley/X-eye brand geometry where applicable. Do not invent a new mark.  
**Risk:** Decorative wallpaper. The image must be excellent enough to exist without brand explanation.

### B05 — FANCY AS FUCK
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** An ordinary cheap meal plated and treated with the visual gravity of high fine dining.  
**Human truth:** ordinary people elevating something unimpressive because, right now, it is fucking excellent.  
**Treatment:** Opulent old-master/still-life or similarly overqualified visual language. Small elegant title if needed.  
**Rule:** The disproportion between subject and treatment does the work.

### B06 — THE CONSULTANT
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** A bloke leaning over another person's barbecue giving obviously unsolicited advice, treated with statesman-level gravitas.  
**Human truth:** everybody knows the person who becomes an instant expert the moment someone else is doing the job.  
**Treatment:** Formal portraiture or another disproportionate prestige language. Tiny `THE CONSULTANT` only if needed.  
**Rule:** The behaviour survives without the costume; treatment must amplify, not invent, the personality.

## Set C — six weapons / calibration set

### C01 — I MEANT TO DO THAT
**Status:** RETAIN / RECALIBRATE  
**Concept:** Woman in immaculate eveningwear standing beside the remains of a spectacularly collapsed folding table at a house party. Drinks, chips and debris on the floor. She still holds an untouched glass and remains completely composed. Nobody looks at camera.  
**Human truth:** maintaining dignity after an obvious fuck-up.  
**Initial treatment:** Harsh 1990s press-flash photography; dirty monochrome.  
**Calibration:** The caption `I MEANT TO DO THAT` risks translating the photograph rather than adding a second observation. Kill it if the image already says it. Find a second joke only if one genuinely exists.

### C02 — shirtfaced COUNTRY CLUB
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** shirtfaced presented as an obnoxiously tasteful old-money sporting club whose prestigious heraldry contains completely ordinary social objects.  
**Initial treatment:** Tiny embroidered lowercase `shirtfaced` over heart; oversized back crest with two plastic stackable chairs crossed like polo mallets, restrained laurels and establishment language. Bone/grey on black.  
**Human truth:** ordinary social life granted absurd institutional prestige.  
**Calibration:** Strong shirtfaced lane. Must use actual brand assets/type where required. It should genuinely pass as expensive clubwear before the stupidity arrives. No funny explanatory copy.

### C03 — BEAUTIFUL FORM
**Status:** RETAIN / RECALIBRATE  
**Concept:** Elegant woman carrying a massive laundry basket on her head with runway-model dignity.  
**Initial treatment:** 1960s European fashion illustration; loose ink, flat colour; image only; asymmetric crop-specific composition.  
**Problem:** Woman + laundry basket is not automatically human truth. Without behaviour underneath it, it is merely surreal fashion art.  
**Recalibration:** Give the action a recognisable reason: refusing to make two trips; both hands already occupied; improvising and carrying the washing with ridiculous dignity.  
**Rule:** The behaviour is the concept. Fashion illustration is the costume.

### C04 — NOT FUCKING LEAVING
**Status:** REJECT EXECUTION / KEEP LEARNING  
**Concept:** Typographic statement built from scale hierarchy: tiny `not`, huge `FUCKING`, microscopic `leaving.`  
**Initial treatment:** Black tank, grey ink, clean editorial typography, no distressing or smiley.  
**Problem:** Attitude typography alone. Any vaguely edgy streetwear brand could print it. There is no specific shirtfaced observation underneath.  
**Learning:** Swearing is punctuation, not the product. Typography cannot rescue the absence of human truth.

### C05 — THE AFTERPARTY
**Status:** STRONG / DEVELOP  
**Concept:** The small archaeological site left after a night out: one high heel, crushed paper party hat, keys, half a birthday cake, dead phone and carefully placed glass of water. No people. No text.  
**Human truth:** everybody recognises the pathetic, strangely tender evidence left at 6:17am.  
**Initial treatment:** Seventeenth-century Dutch still-life gravity; dramatic chiaroscuro; museum-level rendering.  
**Calibration:** One of the strongest ideas in the set. The fine-art language is a costume that disproportionately elevates a real human scene. Avoid generic AI-luxury prettiness; the objects and aftermath must feel observed.

### C06 — WORLD CHAMPION
**Status:** STRONG CONCEPT / RETREATMENT REQUIRED  
**Concept:** Utterly ordinary bloke, exhausted on edge of bed, towel around shoulders, fighting to remove one impossibly stubborn boot as though he has survived twelve rounds.  
**Human truth:** a tiny domestic struggle experienced with the physical and emotional gravity of elite sport.  
**Initial treatment:** 1980s Japanese boxing-poster energy.  
**Problem:** The treatment introduced borrowed personality and began to look like fake Japanese fight merch.  
**Calibration:** The concept survives the COSTUME TEST. Kill the borrowed identity, retain the heroic sporting disproportion. `WORLD CHAMPION` may remain if it does not explain too much.

## Cross-run findings

These concepts produced the calibration now locked in `SHIRTFACED_CREATIVE_BRAIN.md`:

> **Different styles are allowed. Different personalities aren't.**

> **Random incongruity isn't shirtfaced. Human truth + disproportion is.**

And the operating model:

**Concept → Treatment → Personality**

- Concept = human truth, behaviour, relationship, social observation, stupidity or contradiction.
- Treatment = visual language used to disproportionately elevate, undermine or reframe it.
- Personality = always shirtfaced.

Run the COSTUME TEST before promoting any concept into production.

---

## Archive rule

Do not delete rejected concepts merely because they failed. Mark the failure precisely. A bad treatment of a good concept is different from a bad concept, and both are useful training evidence for the future deterministic design engine.

When a concept is promoted into a canonical product/design library, preserve its original ID or cross-reference this archive so the creative lineage is not lost.
