# SHIRTFACED — CAST REFERENCE PROMPTS

**Status:** Production asset. Not canon — `CHARACTERS.md` is canon.
**Purpose:** Generate one persistent reference image per character, once, so scene
prompts stop describing people.
**Date:** 15 August 2026.

---

## Why

`CHARACTER_CONTINUITY.md` §10: *"A recurring campaign character cannot exist only
as a prompt paragraph."* Every scene prompt written before this one described five
people from scratch, which bloated the prompt, buried the moment and produced
slightly different people every time.

One reference per character. Two frames on the same seed — full length, then head
and shoulders. After that a scene prompt is the moment, the place, the light and
the camera, and nothing else.

## Rules for every reference

- Plain mid-grey background, even soft light, 85mm, sharp, full length
- Standing relaxed, looking slightly off camera, not posing
- Base wardrobe only. Scene wardrobe is stated per scene, never here
- **Everything fits.** One good object each at most. They have all made an effort
  and are pretending they haven't
- Every garment completely plain — no logos, graphics, text, embroidery or labels
- No two people share a styling signature

## Family resemblance

Gary, Emma and Grace share deep-set eyes and a heavy brow. Damo shares the brow
with his brother. Nobody else does.

# The prompts

Fourteen references. Two frames each: **A** full length, **B** head and
shoulders, same person, same light, same wardrobe.

**Generate A first, then generate B in the same conversation with A attached.**
The generators in use expose no reusable seed, so the image is what holds the
face. Same rule for every later generation of that character.

Every prompt below is complete on its own — paste it, generate, keep the seed.
None of them names anybody, per `WORLD.md`'s Form rule. The names are headings
in this document, not text in a prompt.

## The shared preamble

Prepend this to every prompt. It is the rules section restated as instruction, so
the per-character text can be nothing but the person.

> Photographic character reference, plain mid-grey seamless background, even soft
> studio light, 85mm lens, sharp throughout, full colour. Standing relaxed,
> weight on one leg, hands loose, looking slightly off camera, not posing. Neutral
> expression with the face at rest. Every garment completely plain — no logos, no
> graphics, no text, no embroidery, no visible labels. Clothes fit properly.
> Photographic, not illustrated. One person only. Full length, head to feet in
> frame, with space above and below.

Frame B, with frame A attached:

> Head and shoulders crop of this same person, same face, same wardrobe, same
> plain mid-grey seamless background, same even soft studio light, 85mm, sharp,
> full colour. Looking slightly off camera, not posing, neutral expression with
> the face at rest. One person only.

## Gary — 43

> A forty-three year old man. Deep-set eyes under a heavy brow. Dry, still, watchful

# What canon does not state

These prompts specify only what `CHARACTERS.md` supports plus what the rules
require. **Everything below was left open on purpose and needs the owner, not a
generator's default.**

| Open | Affects |
|---|---|
| Ethnicity and skin tone | all fourteen |
| Hair colour | all but Sophie |
| Height and build | specified loosely where occupation implies it, guessed nowhere |
| Facial hair | nobody, by omission |
| Tattoos, piercings, glasses | nobody, by omission |

A generator will fill every one of these with its own default and then hold that
default across the whole cast. Decide them once, here, or the references will
have decided them.

`CHARACTERS.md` §6 records that an earlier version invented a cast and was
rewritten. The same discipline applies to faces.
