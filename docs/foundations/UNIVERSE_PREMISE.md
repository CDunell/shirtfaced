# SHIRTFACED — FOUNDATIONAL PREMISE

## This is not a clothing brand.

SHIRTFACED is a persistent Australian social universe.

The clothing is not the product in isolation. The clothing is the visible sign that someone belongs in that universe.

## Hierarchy

1. Attitude — the constant.
2. Worlds — persistent settings within the Shirtfaced universe.
3. Characters — recurring ordinary Australians whose lives continue across worlds.
4. Stories — ongoing continuity rather than isolated campaigns.
5. Products — the club colours naturally worn by the characters.

## Principles

- The universe exists first; products emerge from it.
- WORLD 01 is only one world, not the brand.
- Customers buy into a world they recognise or aspire to belong to.
- Designs are chapters, not standalone jokes.
- Future research focuses on the constitutional design system of the universe rather than individual graphics.
