# SHIRTFACED — CHARACTERS

**Status:** Canon.
**Promoted from:** Hot List item 3 (`docs/shirtfaced-audit.md`), the last open item
in Phase 1 — the character bible.
**Date:** 10 August 2026.
**Decided by:** the owner, in session, from their own family and friends.

---

## 0. Scope & authority

Canon for who the recurring cast are, how they relate, and what they're like.
It sits alongside `UNIVERSE_PREMISE.md` — characters are rung 3 of the
hierarchy, above products and below the worlds they appear in.

Like `BRAND_VOICE.md` and `POSITIONING.md`, this document is not gated by
`PLANNING_CANON_HEADINGS`. It is enforced by review, not code. It does not
change the rule in `WORLD.md`'s Form section: **nobody on this list goes into
a generation prompt by name.** The planner still writes "one bloke," "a
woman," "another mate." This document exists for identity and story work
downstream of generation — captions, continuity, future episodic writing —
not for prompt construction.

---

## 1. The shape

Not a flat friend group. A family, with a friend web attached to it.

**Gary** is the centre — brother to Damo, father to Grace and Emma — and the
rest of the cast connects through one of those three, or through Emma and
Grace's own school and work circles. This is the strongest possible fit for
the brand's own doctrine: nobody gets shirtfaced alone, and here the group
isn't assembled for the photo, it's just who's actually related to who.

---

## 2. The family

**Gary** — real name Chris; goes by Gary. Smart, quiet, sarcastic as fuck,
irreverent, an encyclopaedic knowledge of useless shit. The dry centre
everyone else orbits. Brother to Damo. Father to Emma and Grace.

**Damo** — Gary's younger brother. SK's other half. The chaos agent —
something ill-advised happens to or because of Damo every single time.
Exhausting, beloved, always the first name in the retelling.

A mathematical genius. Ran his own finance company and consulted for some of
the largest companies in Australia. The chaos is not stupidity — it is a very
clever man who has stopped performing seriousness. Separated about five years ago
after eighteen years with the same woman — together from around fifteen, so
she is most of his life to date. The divorce was messy and ugly, and it is
where his view comes from: he came out of it having lost years and decided
not to lose any more. "Living his best life" is not a personality, it is a
position he arrived at the hard way. No children appear in any world.
*(Owner, 15 August 2026.)*

**Damo and SK together** — the power couple who are suburban Aussies at
heart. They swear a lot and laugh more. Neither is impressed by status,
including their own. *(Owner, 15 August 2026.)*

**SK** — Damo's partner. Former New York/London L'Oréal marketing executive.
Goes by initials, not a full first name. Sharp, low patience for excuses,
has run campaigns bigger than this one — which either makes her the
best-qualified person in the group to say an idea is bad, or the least
impressed by any of it.

Born and raised in Brisbane to high school teacher parents. Local girl made
good — the international career is something she went and did, not something
she came from, and she wears it lightly. *(Owner, 15 August 2026.)*

**Grace** — 18+, Gary's daughter, Emma's half-sister. Computer science and
engineering student. The driest humour in the entire cast. Ethan's
girlfriend.

**Ethan** — uni student, IT. Grace's boyfriend. Still slightly on his best
behaviour around the rest of the family, which everyone finds hilarious and
periodically tests on purpose.

**Emma** — Gary's eldest, nursing graduate, wild child. Grace's half-sister.
Casey's best mate.

## 3. The mates

**Casey** — ex party girl, now manages a hair salon. Emma's best mate.

**Sophie** — redhead. School friend of Emma's and Charlotte's. Full range,
"mad as a cut snake." Oliver's girlfriend.

**Oliver** — Sophie's boyfriend.

**Charlotte** — 25, solicitor. School friend of Emma's and Sophie's.

**Mitch** — 26, chef. Brock's mate.

**Brock** — Mitch's mate. The instigator — texts "who's up" at 9pm on a
Tuesday. Loud, generous, financially unreliable, otherwise completely
reliable. If something's happening, he started it.

## 5. Kevin

Not a character. In the owner's real family, every person and dog carries
Kevin as a secret middle name. That's too good to spend on one background
character with a pub-trivia backstory — it plays better as a running device
across the universe: a name that keeps turning up on paperwork, tags, forms,
never explained. Held here as a name until it's decided whether it stays a
device or becomes a person after all.
