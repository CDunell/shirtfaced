## Voice, in one breath

Loud headlines, deadpan body copy. The joke is that nature got confused and nobody —
least of all the creature — is going to acknowledge it. Live site canon: "WHAT HAPPENS
WHEN NATURE GETS CONFUSED?", "MIX IT. LAUGH. WEAR IT.", "MADE FOR LITTLE WEIRDOS",
"YOUR NEW FAVOURITE WEIRDO", "JOIN THE MIX — get new creatures, drops & weird updates."

Do: state absurd things flatly. Treat every hybrid as a completely ordinary animal.
Don't: explain the joke, use rainbow-cute kid-brand energy, or address children.

Named mixups available from the live site: **Platy-Crab** (Platypus × Crab), **Bat-At**
(Bat × Rat), **Spike-Mouse** (Echidna × Mouse), **Duck-Tail** (Duck × Something).

## 3. Five Reel/TikTok scripts

**Script A — "The reveal" (guessing game, repeatable forever)**
- Hook (0–2s): black screen, text: "TWO ANIMALS WENT IN."
- 2–8s: silhouette rotates slowly. Text: "Guess both. No prizes. Just glory."
- 8–12s: hard cut to full-colour creature. Text: "PLATY-CRAB."
- End card: "New mixups monthly. Join the mix → link in bio."

**Script B — "Nature documentary" (deadpan VO, phone footage of the flat art)**
- VO, flat David-Attenborough cadence: "The Spike-Mouse. Found nowhere. Feared by no
  one. It has never once questioned its own existence... and neither should you."
- End card: creature on the tee.

**Script C — "The Mix Machine" (founder BTS)**
- Hook: "I make animals that don't exist. It's a real job."
- Beats: sketch → cross out → sketch → the survivor → the print on a garment.
- Close: "This one's called a Bat-At. A four-year-old will pick it over both of us."

**Script D — "Packing orders" (recurring format)**
- Hook: "Packing order #12 for a kid who chose the weirdest one. Respect."
- Beats: fold, sticker, pack, handwritten "enjoy your weirdo" note.
- Close: order on the post pile.

**Script E — "Which one is your kid?"**
- Four creatures, one line each on screen, parent-voiced VO: "The Plod parent knows.
  The Bat-At parent REALLY knows." CTA: "Tag them."
