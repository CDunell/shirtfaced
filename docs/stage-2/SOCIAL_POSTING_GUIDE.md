# SHIRTFACED — SOCIAL POSTING GUIDE

## Purpose

This is the operating guide for publishing Shirtfaced content to Instagram and TikTok.

The rule is simple:

**content first → Shirtfaced layer second → platform tools last**

The photograph or video must still be worth posting without the branding. The social asset system creates recognition across very different environments without forcing every post into the same dark aesthetic.

---

## 1. Formats

| Use | Canvas | Export |
|---|---:|---|
| Instagram feed photo | 1080 × 1350 | JPG or PNG |
| Instagram carousel | 1080 × 1350 each | JPG or PNG |
| Instagram Reel | 1080 × 1920 | MP4 |
| Instagram Story | 1080 × 1920 | JPG, PNG or MP4 |
| TikTok | 1080 × 1920 | MP4 |
| Reel/TikTok cover | 1080 × 1920 master; keep important content centred for grid crop | JPG/PNG or selected video frame |

Prefer 4:5 for feed posts and 9:16 for motion/story content.

---

## 2. Theme selection

The social system is not a dark-theme system.

Choose the treatment from the content underneath it.

### DARK
Use paper/white marks over:
- pubs
- night streets
- kick-ons
- dark interiors
- night festivals
- silhouettes

### LIGHT
Use ink/black marks over:
- beach
- daylight 4WD
- camping
- bright streets
- daytime BBQs
- pale walls
- open sky

### ADAPTIVE
Use a small ink or paper backing plate when the photograph is visually busy or changes brightness behind the mark.

Use for:
- party flash
- servo lighting
- mixed sun/shadow
- crowds
- headlights
- dawn
- moving video where contrast changes shot to shot

Lime remains the signal/accent colour. It is not the background colour of every post.

---

## 3. What gets branded

Do not put every available graphic on every post.

### Documentary photo
Default: one small lockup OR smiley.

If the image is already recognisably Shirtfaced, publishing it clean is allowed.

### Documentary Reel / TikTok
Default sequence:
1. clean footage
2. optional title bug or timestamp
3. footage continues
4. optional end mark/end card

Do not maintain a permanent frame around the whole video unless the specific piece needs it.

### Drop
More information is allowed because the post has a commercial job.

Use:
- drop panel
- product proof slide
- live banner
- teaser
- end card

### Update / notice
Use a dedicated information card rather than forcing copy over a photograph.

### Carousel
Lead with the strongest image, not an announcement card.

Use later slides for detail, product proof or information. The final slide may carry the stronger Shirtfaced identity/end treatment.

## 5. Editing workflow

Use Figma, CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve or another editor that can layer SVG/PNG assets over media.

### Photo
1. Start with the final photograph.
2. Crop to 1080 × 1350.
3. Choose DARK, LIGHT or ADAPTIVE treatment based on the image.
4. Add the minimum useful Shirtfaced asset.
5. Add copy only if the post needs information.
6. Export.
7. Upload to Instagram.

### Reel / TikTok
1. Edit the actual story first.
2. Work on a 1080 × 1920 timeline.
3. Keep important faces, product and text away from platform UI areas.
4. Add Shirtfaced overlays only where they improve recognition or information.
5. Export the finished MP4.
6. Upload the same master to Instagram Reels and TikTok where appropriate.
7. Add platform-native audio/caption/mentions after upload.

Do not export a TikTok-watermarked file and reuse it on Instagram.

---

## 6. Platform tools

Use Instagram/TikTok for things the platforms are good at:
- audio/music
- post caption
- mentions
- location when useful
- interactive Story tools
- native captions/subtitles when appropriate

Do not rely on platform editors for the permanent Shirtfaced identity. They do not carry the custom brand geometry, typography or spacing system.

---

## 7. First-post checklist

Before publishing:
- strongest photograph/frame first
- correct 4:5 or 9:16 master
- branding readable against the actual image
- no unnecessary frame or graphic furniture
- no accidental third-party logos if the image is meant to be clean
- text inside safe areas
- spelling checked
- audio intentional
- cover frame selected for video
- caption written
- final preview watched/read once in the platform

Then publish it. Do not keep decorating it because there is empty space.

---

## 8. Shirtfaced rule

The system should make a beach photo, a muddy 4WD clip, a Friday-night pub photograph, a servo stop, a festival Reel and a product drop feel like they came from the same label.

It should **not** make them look like they happened in the same place, at the same time, under the same lighting.

Recognition comes from repeated decisions: mark, typography, spacing, placement, lime signal and editorial restraint — not from painting everything black.
