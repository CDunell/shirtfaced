# SHIRTFACED — SOCIAL PUBLISHING RECIPES

## V4 — assembled post recipes

These are not new visual styles. They are repeatable assemblies of the existing V1–V3 assets.

The photograph/video remains the hero. Select LIGHT, DARK or ADAPTIVE from the content, not from the campaign.

---

## RECIPE 01 — DOCUMENTARY PHOTO

**Use for:** beach, pub, 4WD, BBQ, footy, festival, servo, kick-ons, dawn, ordinary life.

**Master:** 1080 × 1350.

### A — Clean
Use the photograph with no overlay.

Choose this when Shirtfaced is already present naturally or when branding would weaken the photograph.

### B — Minimal mark
Use one only:
- `v3/light-corner-mark-4x5.svg`
- `v3/dark-corner-mark-4x5.svg`
- `v3/adaptive-corner-mark-4x5.svg`

### C — Identified frame
Use only when the post needs stronger identity:
- `v3/light-feed-4x5.svg`
- `v3/dark-feed-4x5.svg`
- `v3/adaptive-feed-badge-4x5.svg`

**Never:** add headline copy simply because there is empty space.

---

## RECIPE 02 — DOCUMENTARY REEL / TIKTOK

**Master:** 1080 × 1920 MP4.

**Typical duration:** 6–25 seconds. Longer only when the event genuinely supports it.

### Timeline
**0:00–0:02** — strongest moving moment, clean. Do not waste the opening on a logo animation.

**0:02–0:05** — optional identity/title bug if useful:
- `v3/light-title-bug-9x16.svg`
- `v3/dark-title-bug-9x16.svg`
- `v3/adaptive-reel-badge-9x16.svg`

**Middle** — clean footage. Straight cuts. Held shots. Real movement. Ambient sound can remain.

**Optional once** — timestamp/location/scene information:
- `v2/reel-timestamp-badge-blank.svg`
- `v2/reel-scene-stamp-blank.svg`
- `v3/adaptive-timestamp-9x16.svg`

**Final 0.5–1.5 sec** — either finish naturally or use:
- `v1/reel-endcard-white.svg`
- `v1/reel-endcard-lime.svg`

A brand end card is optional, not mandatory.

**Do not use:** trend transitions, fake film burns, speed ramps, beat-synced reveals or permanent decorative borders.

---

## RECIPE 03 — PHOTO CAROUSEL / MINI STORY

**Master:** 1080 × 1350 per slide.

Recommended 3–7 slides.

### Sequence
1. **Hero** — strongest photograph, preferably clean.
2. **Context** — where we are / who is there.
3. **Moment** — action, interaction, mistake, laugh, movement.
4. **Detail** — product incidental, hand, table, car, pool cue, sand, food, etc.
5. **Aftermath/continuation** — where the night/day went next.
6. **Optional information** — only when there is something useful to say.
7. **Optional close** — `v2/carousel-end-slide-4x5.svg`.

Do not make slide one a title card. The first image earns the swipe.

---

## RECIPE 04 — DROP TEASER

### Story / Reel
Use `v2/story-drop-teaser-9x16.svg`.

Keep the product/reveal area visually dominant. Information should answer only what is necessary: what, when, where.

### Feed
Use documentary/product imagery first. If information must be baked in, use `v1/overlay-drop-panel-4x5.svg`.

Do not turn the teaser into an ecommerce banner.

---

## RECIPE 05 — DROP REVEAL

Recommended carousel:
1. hero lifestyle/product photograph
2. alternate angle / second wearer / context
3. artwork or garment detail
4. `v2/product-proof-slide-4x5.svg`
5. availability / launch information using `v2/drop-live-banner-4x5.svg`
6. optional `v2/carousel-end-slide-4x5.svg`

The garment can be clearer here than in documentary posts because the post has a commercial purpose.

---

## RECIPE 06 — DROP IS LIVE

Use:
- `v2/drop-live-banner-4x5.svg` for feed
- `v2/story-drop-teaser-9x16.svg` or a clean product video for Story/Reel

Keep copy brutally short. Product/drop name, live status, site/action.

---

## RECIPE 07 — UPDATE / ANNOUNCEMENT

Use when the information itself is the content: launch timing, stock status, shipping note, event/update, change of plan.

Feed:
- `v1/notice-update-base-4x5.svg`

Story:
- `v2/story-update-base-9x16.svg`

Do not manufacture announcements to fill the feed. If there is nothing useful to announce, post a photograph instead.

---

## RECIPE 08 — REEL / TIKTOK COVER

The cover must work as a still image first.

### Bright image
`v3/light-cover-badge-4x5.svg`

### Dark image
`v2/reel-cover-badge-4x5.svg`

### Busy image
Use `v3/adaptive-feed-badge-4x5.svg` or crop a clean frame and use no additional treatment.

Keep the meaningful subject near the centre because profile-grid crops differ from the full 9:16 master.

Do not create giant repeated episode titles across every cover. The grid should look like a photographic archive, not Netflix.

---

## RECIPE 09 — PARTY FLASH / SERVO / MIXED LIGHT

Use ADAPTIVE assets rather than trying to guess whether white or black will survive every frame.

Recommended:
- `v3/adaptive-reel-badge-9x16.svg`
- `v3/adaptive-timestamp-9x16.svg`
- `v3/adaptive-corner-mark-4x5.svg`

Backing plates should be small. They exist for contrast, not decoration.

---

## RECIPE 10 — BEACH / 4WD / DAYLIGHT

Do not darken the image to make the brand system fit.

Recommended:
- `v3/light-corner-mark-4x5.svg` for photos
- `v3/light-feed-4x5.svg` when stronger identification is needed
- `v3/light-title-bug-9x16.svg` for motion
- `v3/light-reel-9x16.svg` only when the full overlay earns its place

Natural sky, sand, dust, water and sunlight stay natural.

---

# QUICK DECISION

**Is the image strong enough clean?** → Post it clean.

**Needs only a Shirtfaced fingerprint?** → Corner mark.

**Needs obvious identity?** → Feed/Reel overlay.

**Contrast changes or background is chaotic?** → Adaptive badge.

**Selling/informing?** → Drop/update system.

**Video?** → Story first, graphics second.

---

# V4 ACCEPTANCE RULE

If five posts — beach, muddy 4WD, party flash, dark pub and servo — can sit beside each other and clearly belong to Shirtfaced without looking like five copies of the same template, the system is working.
