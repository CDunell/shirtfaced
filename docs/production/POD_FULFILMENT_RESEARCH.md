## Purpose

Consolidates a GPT-assisted research conversation covering Australian garment
blanks, print-on-demand, local printers, screen printing, outsourced DTF
transfers, in-house equipment and unit economics, so the work does not need to
be repeated or reconstructed from chat.

## Operating rules established during the research

- Do not assume the business is committed to Shopify, WooCommerce, or any
  other ecommerce platform. Do not let a platform integration dictate the
  garment or production strategy.
- Keep supplier, printer, production, fulfilment and ecommerce decisions
  separable.
- Compare complete delivered costs, not selectively quoted garment or print
  prices.
- Cover both major POD companies and Australian local print networks
  (Brisbane, Gold Coast, Melbourne, Sydney, Newcastle/Port Stephens).
- Do not lock shirtfaced into one supplier or method.
- Screen printing once sales justify it; DTF and equipment ownership are
  being investigated for small runs and demos, not adopted yet.
- These are recommendations, not rules imposed on the user.
- Every cost comparison should identify whether it includes garment,
  printing (and number of positions), setup/screens/colour changes, GST,
  inbound freight, pressing labour, packing, customer delivery, storage or
  pick-and-pack, relabelling/neck print/swing tags/bagging, and spoilage or
  size surcharges. **If a supplier price is unclear, it is marked unclear —
  never quietly filled in with an invented number.**

  printers may have trade pricing and can charge handling for supplied
  garments. Always request both printer-supplied and customer-supplied
  (with handling/spoilage terms) pricing.
- **OGO price conflict (unresolved):** OGO's AS Colour 5026 product page
  shows garment A$11, print A$11/side; a newer OGO pricing guide shows
  print at A$13/side. Both state GST-inclusive. AU single-item shipping is
  commonly shown as A$8. **Use the conservative A$13/side figure until
  confirmed directly with OGO.** OGO charges by printed side, not artwork
  size — a small left-chest print costs the same as a large front print.

## POD vs outsourced DTF for demo garments

Use case: garment demos/photography samples before committing to production
stock. Much of the current design range (distressed graphics, halftones,
limited-colour, negative space) suits quality DTF demos well.

Practical quality hierarchy: (1) good screen print for proven designs, (2)
well-pressed premium DTF for small runs/demos/complex art, (3) good POD DTG
where blank/dimensions/consistency are acceptable, (4) cheap DTF or weak POD
is unacceptable regardless of method.

One-off delivered cost on black AS Colour 5026:

| | OGO POD | Outsourced transfer + own pressing |
|---|---:|---:|
| One-side delivered total | A$32 (conservative) | ~A$34–A$40 |
| Chest + back delivered total | A$45 (conservative) | ~A$37–A$44 |

Current demo recommendation: OGO for isolated one-off 5026 demos where its
dimensions/placement work; outsourced DTF + own heat press for Comfort
Colors demos, unusual placement, oversized artwork, exact placement control,
front-and-back combos, and anything needing immediate hands-on QC. A
photographed DTF sample must not be described as screen printed if
production will use a different method.

## Heat-press ownership economics

Strongest first equipment purchase identified: a reliable 40×50cm heat
press, not a DTF printer.

| Setup | Working cost |
|---|---:|
| Very basic 40×50cm press | ~A$399 |
| Better manual press | ~A$1,100 |
| Commercial/semi-automatic | ~A$2,000–A$3,000 |
| Bench/tools/sheets/tape | A$150–A$350 |
| Practical entry setup | A$550–A$1,450 |

Break-even at a conservative A$7/shirt saving: A$550 setup ≈ 79 shirts;
A$1,450 setup ≈ 207 shirts (illustration, not a promise — depends on
transfer size, freight, labour valuation, alternative cost).

Equipment allocation for a A$399 press: A$39.90/garment at 10 pressed,
falling to A$3.99 at 100. For only a few one-off demos, POD can be cheaper
than buying equipment solely for those demos.

## Second-hand DTF printer economics

AU evidence: used A3 printer ~A$2,500 (sold); new Brisbane A3 printer alone
~A$3,290; printer/oven package ~A$4,099–A$4,599; printer/shaker package
~A$4,599–A$5,399; supported commercial systems ~A$5,000–A$35,000+;
higher-grade AU solutions from ~A$9,995.

Realistic second-hand total setup: low estimate A$4,600, safer estimate
A$10,600 (printer, oven/shaker, heat press, RIP/computer, initial
ink/film/powder/cleaner, ventilation/workspace, repair contingency).

Risks: white ink settles/clogs; machines need frequent use and maintenance;
printheads are consumable and can erase the saving; a used unit needs a
nozzle test, live demo, maintenance history, RIP licence status, parts/
support confirmation before purchase.

Working in-house DTF decoration allowance: ~A$5–A$8 for a large print vs
~A$7–A$10 for premade gang sheets pressed — ownership might save only
A$2–A$4/shirt. Break-even: A$4,600 setup at A$4/shirt saving ≈ 1,150 shirts;
at A$3 ≈ 1,534; A$8,000 setup at A$4 ≈ 2,000, at A$3 ≈ 2,667. Excludes
operator time and major repairs.

**Conclusion: do not buy a second-hand DTF printer yet.** Reassess at
~150–250 decorated items/month consistently, or ~A$600–A$1,000/month in
outsourced-transfer invoices, or a deliberate decision to print for third
parties.

## In-house screen-printing economics

Required beyond a manual press: registered press, multiple screens/storage,
exposure unit, washout booth with drainage, pressure washer, flash dryer,
conveyor dryer (or verified alternative), film output, inks/emulsion/
reclaiming/cleaning chemicals, ventilation, work tables, drying/storage
space.

| Capability | Estimated capital |
|---|---:|
| DIY one-colour/manual | A$2,000–A$4,000 |
| Useful four-colour manual shop | A$7,000–A$15,000 |
| Proper production setup | A$20,000–A$50,000+ |

At an illustrative A$35/hr labour value, a 50-shirt one-colour job (coating/
exposure/setup/registration 1.5–3h, printing 1–2h, curing/cleanup/reclaiming
1–2h) gives an effective in-house cost of ~A$5–A$8/shirt before capital
recovery — vs. an outsourced benchmark of ~A$4.90/shirt at 50 units
(one-colour, ex GST, before setup allocation). **In-house does not
automatically win on price at current volume.**

**Conclusion: do not establish in-house screen printing yet.** Becomes
defensible with steady repeat volume, a real operator, and/or a deliberate
second business printing for others.

Two colours, before any separate colour-change fee: 20 → A$33.99, 30 →
A$29.30, 50 → A$24.64, 100 → A$21.84, 200 → A$20.28.

Comfort Colors 1717, one colour (A$18.50 incl. GST blank, assumes no supplied-
garment handling fee): 20 → A$31.87 (profit A$47.13 at A$79), 30 → A$29.57
(A$49.43), 50 → A$25.32 (A$53.68), 100 → A$23.51 (A$55.49), 200 → A$22.90

50→100 saves only ~A$1.81/shirt more, 100→200 only ~A$0.61/shirt more. **50
units is the first meaningful outsourced screen-print breakpoint** for a
proven design — going to 200 to save ~60c/shirt isn't worth it unless demand
supports the inventory.

## Exact quote specification to send every shortlisted supplier

Send every supplier the same spec so quotes are comparable.

**Garments:** AS Colour 5026 Classic, black. Comfort Colors 1717, black
and/or Pepper.

**Quantities:** 25, 50, 100, 250.

**Decoration configurations:**
1. One-colour large back only.
2. One-colour large front only.
3. One-colour small left chest + one-colour large back.
4. Three-colour large back.
5. Three-colour large back + small one-colour left chest, if possible.

**Ask for every charge separately:** garment unit price; print unit price by
position/colour count; screen/setup charge; repeat-run setup charge;
colour-change charge; Pantone matching charge; supplied-garment handling
charge; spoilage allowance/replacement responsibility; 2XL/3XL/4XL
surcharges; sample/strike-off charge; neck relabelling/inside-neck print;
swing tag; folding/individual bagging; inbound/outbound freight to
Brisbane; production lead time; rush charge; storage; pick-and-pack;
direct-to-customer shipping; returns processing; ecommerce/API/CSV/manual-
order capability (as a separate operational question only).

**Quality questions:** plastisol/water-based/discharge/specialty-ink
options; max print dimensions on 5026 and 1717; whether garment-dyed
Comfort Colors needs different ink/curing; whether a physical strike-off
can be approved before the full run; wash-testing/durability process;
registration and placement tolerances; misprint/damaged-supplied-blank
policy.

## Current production path by sales stage

| Proven demand per design | Current best path |
|---:|---|
| One sample/demo | OGO POD (compatible 5026 layouts) or outsourced DTF transfer pressed onto the chosen blank |
| 1–20 | Outsourced DTF transfers, pressed to order; POD valid where cheaper/easier |
| 20–49 | Continue DTF, or get a screen-print quote for simple one-colour designs if demand is firm |
| 50–100 | Outsourced screen-print production run |
| 100–250 | Outsourced automatic screen printing + retail finishing where required |
| Consistent 150–250 decorated units/month across designs | Reassess owning a supported DTF printer |
| Consistent 500+ simple prints/month | Properly evaluate in-house screen printing |

A working commercial ladder, not a restriction — a complex full-colour
design may stay DTF at higher volume; a one-colour design with firm
preorders may move to screens earlier.

## Current recommendation

- Garments: AS Colour 5026 (main), Comfort Colors 1717 (washed/vintage) —
  unchanged, already locked.
- Demo/early sales: OGO for isolated 5026 one-side demos (pending its
  price confirmation); a 40×50cm heat press + outsourced DTF transfers for
  Comfort Colors, exact placement, unusual layouts, small-batch
  replenishment. Do not buy a DTF printer yet. Do not set up in-house
  screen printing yet.
- Proven production: move repeat designs to outsourced screen printing at
  ~50 units, priced by actual artwork colour count and positions (designs
  run 1–3 inks — no single generic "screen-print cost" is adequate).

## Executive summary

shirtfaced has locked AS Colour 5026 as its main premium tee and Comfort
Colors 1717 for washed/vintage releases without committing to any supplier,
POD platform or ecommerce stack. For isolated 5026 demos, OGO currently
appears very competitive at a conservative A$32 delivered for one printed
side and A$45 for chest plus back, though its public pages conflict between
A$11 and A$13 per printed side — unverified. Outsourced DTF transfers plus a
good heat press are the flexible route for Comfort Colors, exact placement,
complex artwork and small runs; buying a DTF printer does not currently pay
back until roughly 1,150–2,700 shirts depending on setup and savings.
Proven one-to-three-colour designs should move to outsourced screen
printing at around 50 units, where the useful cost reduction begins. Next:
confirm OGO live pricing, send an identical quote matrix to six serious
Australian printers, and compare complete garment-to-customer costs with
every inclusion exposed.
