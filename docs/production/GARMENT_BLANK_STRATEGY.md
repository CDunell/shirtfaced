# shirtfaced — garment blank strategy

## Status

**LOCKED — user-approved 13 August 2026**

This is the current product blank direction. It is a range strategy, not an exclusive supplier, production-method, fulfilment, platform or integration decision.

## Locked decision

- **Main range:** AS Colour 5026 Classic
- **Washed/vintage releases:** Comfort Colors 1717
- **Best single all-round choice:** AS Colour 5026 Classic
- **Best-looking specialist choice:** Comfort Colors 1717

## Why

### AS Colour 5026 Classic

Use as the default shirtfaced tee blank.

- 220 GSM combed cotton
- regular, structured fit
- clean and predictable print surface
- strong Australian availability
- appropriate for the premium core range

### Comfort Colors 1717

Use where the garment-dyed, worn-in finish is part of the product concept.

- approximately 207 GSM ring-spun cotton
- relaxed fit
- soft-washed, garment-dyed finish
- suited to washed, vintage and specialist releases
- colour and finish variation are part of its character

## Application rule

Choose the blank by product intent:

- standard premium shirtfaced tee → AS Colour 5026 Classic
- washed or vintage-led shirtfaced tee → Comfort Colors 1717

This decision does **not** prohibit other blanks, suppliers, printers, production runs, POD arrangements, ecommerce platforms or fulfilment models. Alternatives remain available when a product, fit, colour, technique, stock position or commercial requirement justifies them.
