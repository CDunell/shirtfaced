# AI Capability Matrix

## Image generation

Currently possible:
- photorealistic documentary scenes
- editorial stills
- product photography
- blank-garment scenes
- product mockups
- transparent artwork
- object studies
- background plates
- empty locations
- scene variants
- crop and aspect-ratio variants
- campaign stills from a locked visual universe

Current limitations:
- exact recurring-character consistency
- exact garment geometry across many images
- exact logo placement
- reliable text rendering
- precise product construction details
- continuity across large scene sequences
- clean hands, props and interactions in complex groups
- fully trustworthy Australian authenticity without strong direction

## Image editing

Possible:
- object removal
- object addition
- clothing replacement
- colour changes
- background replacement
- day-to-night conversion
- weather variation
- outpainting
- reframing
- extending negative space
- lighting changes
- removing commercial logos
- scene cleanup

Limitations:
- exact preservation of all untouched details
- garment identity through repeated edits
- small text and labels
- consistent anatomy through multi-step edits
- maintaining the same face across large batches
- controlled typography

## Image to video

Possible:
- camera push-in
- slow handheld drift
- parallax
- subtle body motion
- blinking and breathing
- neon flicker
- rain
- steam
- smoke
- headlights
- traffic movement
- crowd ambience
- looping cinemagraph-style motion
- short scene extensions
- vertical Reel and TikTok clips

Limitations:
- identity drift
- garment drift
- hand and object interaction
- readable text
- long continuous action
- believable multi-person conversations
- continuity across separate generated clips
- stable product details
- consistent physics
- documentary realism beyond short clips without careful finishing

## Native video generation

Possible:
- short original scenes
- atmospheric B-roll
- establishing shots
- transitional footage
- environmental motion
- stylised or documentary-like single shots

Limitations:
- reliable 30–90 second continuity
- recurring cast
- exact location continuity
- exact clothing continuity
- product placement consistency
- multi-shot narrative logic
- speech sync
- production-safe text and logos

## Video editing

AI-assisted tools can:
- remove objects
- extend frames
- reframe horizontally and vertically
- stabilise
- interpolate frames
- generate captions
- translate captions
- create rough cuts
- detect highlights
- clean audio
- reduce noise
- upscale
- generate filler frames
- change backgrounds in controlled shots

Limitations:
- release-ready taste
- exact pacing
- visual continuity
- documentary restraint
- premium finishing
- brand-specific judgment

## Audio

Possible:
- generated ambience
- pub room tone
- traffic
- footsteps
- rain
- neon hum
- distant laughter
- crowd beds
- glass and bottle sounds
- sound cleanup
- stem separation
- looping and extension

Limitations:
- licensing certainty varies by platform and provider
- generic generated audio can feel artificial
- voice and crowd realism may fail under close scrutiny

## Voice

Possible:
- text-to-speech
- Australian accents
- emotional delivery
- voice cloning with permission
- multi-speaker dialogue
- radio-style voice
- narration

Current SHIRTFACED position:
- no decision that the brand needs a voice
- faceless does not automatically mean narrated
- silence, music, captions and environmental sound may better preserve the documentary world

## Music

Possible:
- generation
- extension
- looping
- stem separation
- tempo adjustment
- mood matching

Limitations:
- commercial licensing must be verified per provider
- platform business-account music libraries may be restricted
- trend audio is not automatically commercially usable
