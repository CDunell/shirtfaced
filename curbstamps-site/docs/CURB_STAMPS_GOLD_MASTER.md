# CURB STAMPS — GOLD MASTER

## Canvas

- **3692 × 4800 px**
- **300 DPI**
- Physical size: **12.31 × 16 in**
- Centre X: **1846 px**

## Vertical system

- **0–1250 px:** collar/chest clear space
- **1250–2500 px:** creature zone
- **2580–2850 px:** character name zone
- **2920–3070 px:** CURB STAMPS zone
- **Below ~3150 px:** clear

## Creature

- Maximum bounding box: **2300 × 1200 px**
- Safe box: **X 696–2996 / Y 1250–2500**
- Preserve each creature's natural proportions.
- Never stretch artwork to fill the box.
- Optically centre asymmetric creatures rather than blindly centring their bounding boxes.
- Narrow or tall creatures remain narrow or tall.

## Character name

- Target size: **160 pt**
- **3–5 letters**
- Uppercase
- Centred
- Tracking target: **+40 to +70**
- Do not scale different names to equal widths.

## CURB STAMPS wordmark

- Target size: **65 pt**
- Uppercase
- Centred
- Tracking target: **+180 to +240**

## Spacing

- Creature → character name: **130–170 px** from the creature's actual lowest visible point.
- Character name → CURB STAMPS: **60–75 px**.
- Optical consistency takes priority over mechanical bounding-box consistency.

## Production rule

Final POD artwork contains only the **cream-white creature and typography on transparency**. Guides are production aids only and must be hidden or removed before export.
