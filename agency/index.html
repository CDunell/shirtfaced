What's wrong with most venue merch

Logo-slapped

Same crest, same font, dropped onto a blank tee. Bought once as a favour, worn once, forgotten.

Bought as inventory

Boxes of stock sitting in a back room, tying up cash before a single unit sells.

Not actually designed

Nobody on staff is an illustrator, so "the merch guy" ends up doing it in Canva at 11pm.

How it works

01

Brief

Short call or written brief — your venue, your regulars, what's already working (a beer name, a nickname, an in-joke your locals already say).

02

Design

Original illustration built around that brief — not a template, not a stock font on your crest.

03

Sample & proof

You see and approve the actual print before anything is produced at volume.

04

Production & fulfilment

We handle printing, stock and shipping. You don't carry inventory or field customer service tickets.
