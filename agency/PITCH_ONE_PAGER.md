## The pitch, in one line

Original, illustration-led merch for your venue — designed, produced and fulfilled by
us, at no upfront cost to you.

## The problem we're pitching against

Most venue merch is a logo stuck on a blank tee, bought as inventory, designed by
whoever's free that week. It gets worn once and forgotten.

## What we do instead

1. **Brief** — a short call about your venue, your regulars, what they already joke
   about.
2. **Design** — original illustration built around that brief, not a template.
3. **Sample & proof** — you approve the actual print before any volume run.
4. **Production & fulfilment** — we handle printing, stock and shipping. You carry no
   inventory and field no customer-service tickets.

## Pricing model

| Model | Upfront cost to venue | How we're paid |
|---|---|---|
| Merch-as-a-service | $0 | Share of each sale, no minimums |
| Design only, you produce | Flat design fee | One-off |
| Capsule collab | By quote | Joint release, split by agreement |

Exact rates to be confirmed per brief.

## Why this beats the standard brewery-merch vendor

Every incumbent brewery-merch supplier (Brewery Branding Co, Crestline, Bout It Merch,
and similar) is a logo-print shop — competent production, no original design capability.
We start from illustration, the way a design studio would, not a print shop.
