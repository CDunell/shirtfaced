# Source Prompt

Variation instruction: That one again.

Photorealistic documentary photograph.

Australia.

Friday night.

9:43pm.

Six ordinary Australians in their mid-twenties to mid-thirties walking together down a slightly wet suburban entertainment strip between venues.

Three men.

Three women.

Some are couples.

Some are lifelong mates.

Nobody is introduced.

Nobody is the hero.

The group feels like they have known each other for years.

Nobody is posing.

Nobody acknowledges the camera.

Nobody is performing.

One bloke is halfway through telling a story.

A woman beside him is laughing uncontrollably.

Another woman has her arm linked through her partner's as they're walking.

One bloke carries four schooners because everyone else has their hands full.

Someone is stealing hot chips from someone else's takeaway bag.

One woman has thrown her hoodie over another friend's shoulders because she's cold.

One couple have drifted half a step ahead of the group.

Everyone else is naturally spread across the footpath.

The atmosphere is optimistic.

The night is just getting started.

Everything still feels possible.

You can almost hear someone say,

"Where to next?"

The street is alive without feeling crowded.

Warm pub light spills across the footpath.

Neon signs glow in nearby windows.

Traffic passes behind them.

Headlights reflect across slightly damp asphalt.

A taxi waits at the lights.

A couple cross the street in the distance.

Everything feels completely ordinary.

Everything feels completely Australian.

Photographed from across the street by another friend walking slightly behind the group.

35mm documentary photography.

50mm lens.

Eye level.

Kodak Portra 400.

Natural film grain.

Available light only.

No HDR.

No artificial cinematic colour grading.

Slight motion blur from people walking.

Imperfect framing.

One person is partially cropped by the edge of the frame.

A passing pedestrian briefly obscures part of the foreground.

The photograph feels accidental.

It feels like someone's favourite image from a night everyone still talks about years later.

Leave natural opportunities for future Shirtfaced product placement.

One person wears a plain vintage-washed black heavyweight t-shirt with an unobstructed chest.

Another wears a plain black heavyweight hoodie, unzipped.

Another wears a plain black cap with a clean front panel.

Another has a plain black hoodie tied around their waist.

Another carries a plain black tote bag over one shoulder.

All garments are completely blank.

No logos.

No branding.

No graphics.

No visible labels.

Leave every product placement naturally visible without anyone intentionally displaying it.

The photograph should be good enough that someone would post it on Instagram even if everyone was wearing plain black clothing.
