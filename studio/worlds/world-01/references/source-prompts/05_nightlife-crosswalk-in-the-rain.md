# Source Prompt

Photorealistic documentary photograph.

Australia.

Friday night.

10:54pm.

A busy pedestrian crossing between pubs on a suburban entertainment strip.

The lights have just turned green.

Eight ordinary Australians in their mid-twenties to mid-thirties cross together.

Four men.

Four women.

Nobody is posing.

Nobody acknowledges the camera.

The group has naturally stretched across the crossing.

One couple have drifted half a step ahead without realising.

One bloke is animatedly telling a story with both hands.

The woman beside him is laughing so hard she has to grab his shoulder.

Another bloke has somehow ended up carrying everyone's jackets.

One woman is checking directions on her phone while still walking.

Someone reaches across to steal a chip from another person's takeaway bag.

One mate points excitedly at another group across the road after recognising someone.

Nobody stops walking.

Nobody looks organised.

Nobody is modelling.

The crossing simply caught them in the middle of a great night.

Traffic waits patiently.

Headlights create long reflections across the slightly wet road.

Warm light spills from pubs and restaurants on both sides of the street.

Neon signs glow in nearby windows.

A tram or bus passes in the distance.

A taxi waits first in line at the lights.

A cyclist slips quietly through the intersection.

Another unrelated group crosses behind them.

Everything feels busy without feeling crowded.

Everything feels completely ordinary.

Everything feels unmistakably Australian.

Photographed from the opposite footpath by another friend who crossed slightly earlier.

35mm documentary photography.

50mm lens.

Kodak Portra 400.

Available light only.

Natural film grain.

Slight motion blur from people walking.

Slightly underexposed.

No HDR.

No cinematic colour grading.

Imperfect framing.

One member of the group is partially cropped by the edge of the frame.

A passing pedestrian briefly obscures part of the foreground.

The photograph feels accidental rather than composed.

It feels like somebody's favourite photograph from a night everyone still remembers.

Leave natural opportunities for future Shirtfaced product placement.

One person wears a plain vintage-washed black heavyweight t-shirt with a clean unobstructed chest.

Another wears a plain black heavyweight hoodie, unzipped.

Another wears a plain black cap with a clean front panel.

Another has a plain black hoodie tied around their waist.

Another carries a plain black tote bag over one shoulder.

Another wears a plain black overshirt open over a black tee.

All garments are completely blank.

No logos.

No branding.

No graphics.

No visible labels.

Leave every product placement naturally visible without anyone intentionally displaying it.

The photograph should be good enough that someone would post it on Instagram even if everyone was wearing plain black clothing.
