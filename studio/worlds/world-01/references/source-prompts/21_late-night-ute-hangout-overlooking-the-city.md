# Source Prompt

Photorealistic documentary photograph.

Australia.

Saturday morning.

3:18am.

A quiet suburban lookout overlooking the city.

The kick-ons have paused for five minutes.

Nobody suggested it.

Everyone simply ended up here.

Seven ordinary Australians in their mid-twenties to mid-thirties.

Four men.

Three women.

Nobody is posing.

Nobody notices the camera.

Nobody is looking at the view because they've all seen it before.

The view is simply somewhere to keep talking.

One bloke sits casually on the edge of a white Australian tray-back ute telling the second half of the story nobody let him finish earlier.

A woman is laughing so hard she's almost spilling her drink.

Another sits cross-legged on the concrete barrier eating leftover chips from a plain unbranded bag.

One mate is leaning comfortably against the aluminium tray wearing a plain black cap, naturally facing towards the camera so the entire front panel is clearly visible.

Another has climbed into the open alloy tray and is lying on his back looking at the stars.

A couple quietly share earbuds for a few seconds while everyone else keeps talking over the top.

One woman has another friend's plain black hoodie draped over her shoulders because the air has turned cold.

Nobody is on their phone.

Nobody is checking the time.

Nobody wants the night to end.

The atmosphere feels peaceful.

Hopeful.

Content.

Like everyone secretly knows they'll remember this moment more than the pub.

The city glows softly below.

Orange streetlights stretch to the horizon.

The skyline is distant and secondary.

A faint breeze moves loose hair and hoodie drawstrings.

The road behind is almost empty.

One car quietly passes in the distance.

Everything feels completely ordinary.

Everything feels unmistakably Australian.

Photographed from beside another parked car by the last friend walking over with food.

35mm documentary photography.

50mm lens.

Kodak Portra 400.

Available light only.

Natural film grain.

Slightly underexposed.

No HDR.

No cinematic colour grading.

Natural motion blur from people laughing and moving.

Imperfect framing.

The bonnet of another parked car partially occupies the foreground.

One member of the group is slightly cropped by the edge of the frame.

The photograph feels accidental rather than composed.

It feels like the photograph somebody keeps framed because it reminds them exactly what friendship felt like in their twenties.

Vehicle:

A white Australian tray-back ute.

Open aluminium alloy tray.

Single cab or extra cab.

2000–2015 era.

Tradie specification.

Slightly used but well cared for.

Never an American pickup truck.

Never an enclosed pickup tub.

Never a lifted lifestyle 4WD.

Product rotation:

The black cap is the hero product in this image.

The wearer naturally faces generally towards the camera so the complete front panel remains clearly visible.

The cap is being worn normally, never displayed.

Another person wears a plain vintage-washed black heavyweight t-shirt with a clean unobstructed chest.

Another wears a plain black heavyweight hoodie.

Another has a plain black hoodie tied around their waist.

Another carries a plain black tote bag.

Everyone else wears ordinary casual clothing in varied colours and fabrics.

The Shirtfaced garments are the only deliberately black clothing.

CRITICAL:

Every Shirtfaced garment must be completely blank.

No logos.

No branding.

No graphics.

No printed text.

No embroidery.

No visible labels.

Every takeaway bag, drink cup, food container and food packaging must be completely generic.

No readable branding.

No commercial logos.

No visible trademarks anywhere in the image.

The photograph should be good enough that someone would post it on Instagram even if everyone was wearing plain black clothing.
