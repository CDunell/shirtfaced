# Source Prompt

Photorealistic documentary photograph.

Australia.

Friday night.

12:07am.

A brightly lit suburban service station.

Not the destination.

Just a stop on the way to whatever happens next.

Six ordinary Australians in their mid-twenties to mid-thirties.

Three men.

Three women.

Nobody is posing.

Nobody acknowledges the camera.

Everyone has naturally drifted into their own small job without anyone planning it.

One bloke is filling the ute while still talking to everyone else.

Another is washing the windscreen even though it barely needs it.

One woman walks back from the servo carrying an absurd armful of snacks, energy drinks and bags of ice.

Another has found a stray shopping basket and is wheeling it around for absolutely no reason.

One bloke is sitting casually on the tailgate eating a hot pie straight from the paper bag.

A woman quietly steals a bite before he notices.

Someone is laughing so hard they have to stop walking.

One couple are leaning against the ute talking while everyone else keeps moving around them.

Nobody is rushing.

Nobody looks like a model.

Nobody is performing.

It feels like the little pause everyone remembers before the rest of the night.

The fluorescent servo canopy throws clean white light over everything.

The forecourt is slightly wet from earlier rain.

Fuel pumps glow.

Price boards illuminate the background.

The convenience store spills warm light through large glass windows.

Cars pull in and out.

Headlights sweep across the concrete.

A truck idles at the diesel bowsers.

A stranger fills their car in the distance.

Everything feels completely ordinary.

Everything feels unmistakably Australian.

Photographed from across the forecourt by another friend waiting beside their own car.

35mm documentary photography.

50mm lens.

Kodak Portra 400.

Available light only.

Natural film grain.

No HDR.

No cinematic colour grading.

Slight motion blur from people walking.

Slightly underexposed.

Imperfect framing.

One person is partially cropped by the edge of frame.

A fuel bowser partially obscures part of the group.

The photograph feels accidental rather than composed.

It feels like someone's favourite memory from a night everyone still talks about.

Leave natural opportunities for future Shirtfaced product placement.

One person wears a plain vintage-washed black heavyweight t-shirt with a clean unobstructed chest.

Another wears a plain black heavyweight hoodie, unzipped.

Another wears a plain black cap with a clean front panel.

Another has a plain black hoodie tied around their waist.

Another carries a plain black tote bag over one shoulder.

All garments are completely blank.

No logos.

No branding.

No graphics.

No visible labels.

Leave every product placement naturally visible without anyone intentionally displaying it.

The photograph should be good enough that someone would post it on Instagram even if everyone was wearing plain black clothing.
