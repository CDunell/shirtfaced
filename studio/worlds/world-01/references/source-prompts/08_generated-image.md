# Source Prompt

Photorealistic documentary photograph.

Australia.

Friday night.

11:52pm.

A crowded suburban house party.

Not the loud room.

Not the dance floor.

The kitchen.

Eight ordinary Australians in their mid-twenties to mid-thirties.

Four men.

Four women.

Nobody is posing.

Nobody acknowledges the camera.

The kitchen has naturally become party headquarters.

One bloke is making drinks for everyone despite nobody asking him to.

Another is leaning against the fridge telling a story that keeps getting interrupted.

One woman is laughing so hard she has to brace herself against the kitchen bench.

Another quietly steals a handful of chips from an open packet before anyone notices.

One couple are squeezed into the corner talking like they're the only two people in the house.

Someone reaches into the freezer looking for more ice.

Someone else is unsuccessfully trying to twist the cap off a bottle while still holding a conversation.

Half-empty pizza boxes.

An esky overflowing with ice.

Paper plates.

Open bags of chips.

Keys thrown on the bench.

Phones charging from every available powerpoint.

A Bluetooth speaker sits on the windowsill.

Music can almost be heard through the photograph.

Warm kitchen lighting mixes with cooler light from outside through the open sliding door.

People move through the background carrying drinks.

Someone walks past the doorway completely out of focus.

The house feels lived in.

Comfortable.

Messy in the best possible way.

The night is still climbing.

Nobody wants to leave.

Everything feels completely ordinary.

Everything feels unmistakably Australian.

Photographed from the dining room by another friend who happened to look up at exactly the right moment.

35mm documentary photography.

50mm lens.

Kodak Portra 400.

Available light only.

Natural film grain.

No HDR.

No cinematic colour grading.

Slight motion blur.

Slightly underexposed.

Imperfect framing.

The kitchen island blocks part of the foreground.

One person is cropped by the edge of frame.

The photograph feels discovered rather than composed.

It feels like the image everyone rediscovers five years later and immediately remembers that night.

Leave natural opportunities for future Shirtfaced product placement.

One person wears a plain vintage-washed black heavyweight t-shirt with a clean unobstructed chest.

Another wears a plain black heavyweight hoodie.

Another wears a plain black cap sitting on the kitchen bench beside them.

Another has a plain black hoodie tied around their waist.

Another carries a plain black tote bag dropped beside the esky.

All garments are completely blank.

No logos.

No branding.

No graphics.

No visible labels.

Leave every product placement naturally visible without anyone intentionally displaying it.

The photograph should be good enough that someone would post it on Instagram even if everyone was wearing plain black clothing.
