# THE BIG NIGHT

*World 01. One Friday. Gary, Damo, Brock, Emma, Casey.*

*The readable cut. `STORY_ARC.md` is the production version.*

---

### 4:52pm — the beer garden

The garden is full of people who came straight from work.

Brock got here first and has already ordered for everyone.

He didn't ask anybody what they wanted and he's got all five right.

The table seats four. There are five of them.

Damo goes to the next table and takes a stool off it. It scrapes the whole way
back. Nobody at that table looks up.

Casey slides two glasses down to make the gap before he gets there.

Gary and Emma stand at the end waiting, shoulder to shoulder. Neither of them
says anything.

Brock puts a beer in Emma's hand. She didn't ask for one.

"Two," Gary says. "That's it."

Nobody answers him.

The stool is too low for the table. Damo sits on it anyway.

---

### 9:26pm — the side street

They come out through the side gate in ones and twos.

Damo is carrying the stool.

Nobody saw him take it out. Nobody asks.

He walks backwards up the footpath so he can keep talking. Brock is holding
three phones and somebody's jacket. Casey is closing on the stool and hasn't
decided whether she can be bothered.

Gary and Emma walk at the back, half a step apart. His shirt is open and it's
the only loose thing on the street.

Damo walks into the milk crates.

He picks the stool back up without stopping the sentence.

There's stencilling on the workshop wall behind them. Nobody looks at it and it's
half out of frame.

---

### 10:31pm — the other door

They don't get in.

The bloke on the door goes down the line and stops at Damo. He doesn't say much.
He doesn't have to.

Nobody argues.

They're still standing there when Brock whistles from round the side.

Nobody saw him go.

There's a bistro door propped on a milk crate and he's holding it with his foot.

They're in.

---

### 11:05pm — the band

A four-piece in the back room playing something everybody in the room knows.

Damo is at the pool table. It's his shot.

He is not taking his shot.

He's got the cue up like a mic stand and he's doing the entire chorus at the band,
facing them, at volume.

The bloke he's playing has been waiting with his own cue for a while now and has
said nothing.

The stool is on the pool table. There's a beer on the stool.

Brock is singing too. Brock does not know this song.

Casey is filming it and not singing.

Emma has both arms round a woman she met eleven minutes ago and they're doing the
chorus together like they've known each other since school.

Gary is at the far end of the room with his back to the wall, not singing, holding
a beer, watching all of it.

Nobody in the band has noticed.

---

### 11:20pm — the rain

It comes down without warning and it comes down hard.

Damo makes the awning first and takes the stool with him.

Casey gets under a shopfront with her jumper pulled up over her head.

Brock stops dead in the middle of the footpath, puts his arms out and lets it
happen to him.

Emma doesn't run. She stands out in it with her head back.

Gary walks. He doesn't hurry and he doesn't get under anything.

It stops as fast as it started.

Casey comes out from under the shopfront and does Emma's hair for her, properly,
with a tie off her own wrist. Emma stands still for it and keeps talking to
somebody else the whole time.

Damo has not come out from under the awning.

---

### 11:32pm — the drain

The grate's running from the rain.

Emma's phone is in it.

Not near it. In it.

Brock pulls his hoodie off over his head and hands it to Casey before he gets down
there, because he's not an idiot.

Then he's flat on the road with his arm down to the shoulder. Casey is holding the
hoodie and a torch. Damo is on the footpath giving advice and is not helping.

Gary walks off up the street and comes back with a wire coat hanger.

Nobody asks where from.

The coat hanger doesn't work.

So they lift the grate.

All five of them, on a wet road, at twenty-five to twelve. It takes a while and it
weighs what a grate weighs and it comes up.

Brock has his hoodie back on and one arm of a black t-shirt is ruined underneath
it.

Emma gets her phone out of eighteen inches of stormwater.

It's dead.

She puts it in her pocket.

---

### 1:17am — the servo

Still wet. The whole canopy doubled in the asphalt.

Four of them come around the corner onto the forecourt.

They came for ice.

They leave with three bags of it, forty dollars of servo food nobody is going to
eat, two chargers for phones that aren't flat, and a pair of servo sunglasses.

Brock buys the sunglasses. It is seventeen minutes past one in the morning.

He puts them on and does not take them off again for six hours.

Damo has the ice freezer open with the stool at his feet.

Brock pulls his hoodie off over his head for the second time tonight and holds it
out.

Emma takes it by the cuff.

He's in a filthy t-shirt now and it's one in the morning.

He picks up the ice because he's the coldest person there.

---

### 2:35am — outside the takeaway

The sign above them has a dead letter in it.

The bloke at the counter puts the bag on the ledge and goes back to the fryer.

Four of them on the kerb. The stool stands on the footpath between them with the
food on it.

There are five containers.

One of them stays shut.

Casey has her sleeves pulled down over her hands. Emma is in a hoodie that's too
big for her. Brock hasn't mentioned being cold.

After a while Damo opens the last one and eats it.

Casey takes a chip out of it without looking at it.

---

### 3:41am — the front gate

The bag of ice is mostly water now. Brock holds it away from his leg.

There's a tray-back ute parked in the residents' spot with the tray down.

Damo doesn't make the gate.

He's in the tray. Flat on his back with the stool on his chest and both arms round
it.

He is not going anywhere and he is not letting go of it either.

Emma props the gate open with her foot.

Casey stops at the letterbox bank. There's a name on flat six, done in label
tape. She reads it and doesn't say anything.

Two floors up a door is open and there's noise coming out of it.

Brock looks back at him.

"Nah," Casey says. "Leave him. He's fine."

They all go up.

---

### 4:26am — somebody's kitchen

Casey has been through a freezer that isn't hers and come out with a bag of party
pies.

She hands them to Brock, who has never turned anything down in his life.

He's wearing a bucket hat that isn't his either.

Emma is standing by with a plate. Her boots are off.

Damo got up eventually. He's sitting on the stool in front of the oven telling
Brock how to do it.

Casey is in the doorway with her arms folded, watching the thing she started.

He puts the tray down on the stool because there's no bench left.

Emma's phone turns itself back on at twenty to five on top of the fridge.

Nobody was expecting that.

---

### 5:58am — the balcony

The flat has gone quiet behind them.

Four of them on the concrete with their backs to the wall, legs out.

The stool is in the middle of them with nothing on it.

Nobody sits on it.

Emma has the hood up and her boots beside her. Casey is against her shoulder and
neither of them is moving.

Brock is still in a t-shirt and still in the sunglasses.

The balcony light is still on and doing nothing.

It gets light. The sun doesn't come up yet.

Two floors down a bloke comes out onto his balcony in his jocks and tells them to
shut up.

They go quiet.

About forty seconds.

He's up there with them by twenty past six.

Nobody asks him anything and he doesn't offer.

---

### 7:20am — the corner pub

Shut. Chairs upside down on the tables under the awning.

They come up the footpath the way they came.

Damo stands the stool against the doors, upright, on its feet.

Casey moves it two inches and leaves it.

Emma is carrying her boots. Brock is in the bucket hat and the sunglasses.

Nobody knocks.

On the way past, Damo lifts a different one off the stack under the awning and
keeps walking.

Nobody says anything.
