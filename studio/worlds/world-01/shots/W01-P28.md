# SHOT SPECIFICATION — W01-P28 — "THE BAND"

**Scene:** `W01-P28`, World 01 live-music sequence.  
**Event time:** Friday 11:05pm.  
**Status:** Active production specification.

## Scene

The back room of an Australian pub is packed shoulder-to-shoulder while a small live band plays on a low stage. Damo, Emma and Brock are three mates inside the crowd having a genuinely good night: singing, laughing at each other and getting carried by the chorus.

The band and anonymous crowd are the world around them, not supporting extras arranged around a hero. Attention stays distributed through the room. Nobody acknowledges the camera. There is no stunt, pool-table action, hero gag or posed finale.

The visual target is restrained premium streetwear campaign documentary content captured as accidental vertical phone footage. Wardrobe, faces, hair, builds, age, lighting and pub geography stay continuous across observations.

## Production image model

W01-P28 uses independent native vertical shot masters. Each master is already the intended camera observation and is animated directly by Veo. The scene may hold additional candidate frames, with a maximum of five approved production masters at one time.

The stable shot identifier is the descriptive master slug. Sequence is controlled separately by `sort_order`; letters are not part of the production identity.

## Veo production rule

Each approved master owns an editable shot-specific motion prompt in Studio. The reusable Veo compiler supplies temporal, camera, crowd, first-frame and physical-state invariance; the shot prompt describes only the motion beat that belongs to that frame.

**First-frame orientation is binding.** A motion prompt must never instruct a visible person to turn toward the band, stage, camera or another subject unless that rotation is already the intended action visible from the supplied frame. For `trio-wide`, `damo-emma`, `trio-together` and `trio-chorus`, the trio remains oriented inward toward one another. Only `band-room` is a stage-facing observation.

Generate and review one take at a time. A take is raw source footage, not a required six-second story unit. The finished edit may use only the strongest one-to-three seconds from a generation.

Across every take:

- preserve the supplied first frame as visual truth;
- preserve first-frame body orientation and blocking unless the shot-specific motion explicitly requires a small local change;
- keep camera motion to subtle handheld phone sway, crowd bumps and minor corrective settling;
- preserve exact identities, wardrobe, object scale, lighting and room density;
- keep the band as the performance source and the crowd as independent background life;
- do not invent sweat, wet skin, soaked clothing, heat distress, grime, injuries or progressive appearance-state changes;
- do not invent hats, tattoos, jewellery or new props;
- do not create a beginning, escalation, punchline, toast, staged embrace or resolved ending;
- nobody looks into the lens.

Generated audio is discarded. Final sound is built continuously in post from pub, band and crowd ambience.

## Editorial shape

The working sequence is:

`trio-wide → damo-emma → trio-together → band-room → trio-chorus`

This is a guide to coverage, not a demand to use every full take. The scene should feel like several observations from the same ongoing chorus rather than five separate staged events.
