## Before you send anything

Three things are unresolved and each one changes what a producer is quoting on.

1. **The hero scene has no identifier in this repository.** `SOUNDTRACK.md` §0
   sets this out. The 12.5-second section is the centre of the commission, and
   the outreach brief currently describes its *dramatic function* rather than
   pointing at a scene, because there is no scene to point at. If a picture
   reference exists elsewhere, add it — producers quote better against picture.
2. **The owning entity is not named.** Every assignment in
   [`04_CHAIN_OF_TITLE.md`](04_CHAIN_OF_TITLE.md) needs a legal entity as
   assignee. `[OWNING ENTITY]` is left as a placeholder throughout.
3. **Counsel has not drafted the assignment.** Do not send document 2 — the
   paid demo request — before there is a test agreement that assigns the demo
   and its underlying writing. That is the document doing the actual work.

Placeholders in the sendable documents are written as `[LIKE THIS]`. Search for
`[` before sending.

---

## What not to send

The budget in `SOUNDTRACK.md` §10 — target, ceiling and contingency — is
internal. The outreach brief asks producers for a fixed quote against a defined
scope. Naming the ceiling first converts it into the price.

Likewise the scorecard weightings: publishing them invites producers to optimise
against the rubric rather than write the better song.
