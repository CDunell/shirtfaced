# Selection scorecard — 100 points

**Internal. Do not send to producers.**

One sheet per demo. Two settings from the same producer are scored as one
submission — score the stronger setting, and note whether the weaker one was
genuinely different.

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | Points | Pass question | Score |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | Hook recall / crowd usability | 25 | Can a new listener reproduce the four-hit release after one play? | |
| 2 | Original identity | 20 | Does it avoid a recognisable catalogue shadow in melody, rhythm, harmony, lyric and sound? | |
| 3 | Premium contemporary production | 15 | Does it feel current and tactile without genre costume? | |
| 4 | Hero-sync dramatic fit | 15 | Does the 12.5-second section plausibly animate a whole room? | |
| 5 | Vocal casting / Australian truth | 10 | Does it sound lived-in, inclusive and unforced? | |
| 6 | Editability / deliverables | 10 | Are clean handles, stems and alternates designed in? | |
| 7 | Rights readiness | 5 | Are contributors, inputs and society status fully disclosed? | |
| | **Total** | **100** | | |

---

## Criterion 1 is tested, not judged

Twenty-five points is the largest single weighting and it is the one most easily
faked by scoring it from memory after four listens. Test it instead:

Play the demo once to someone who has never heard it. Then ask them to shout the
four hits. Record what they actually produce.

- All four, in order, correct words — **21–25**
- All four, order or a word wrong — **15–20**
- Three of four — **8–14**
- Two or fewer — **0–7**

Do this with at least two people per demo. If they disagree, take the lower.

---

## Similarity screen — hard gate, scored separately

Panel of at least three listeners, each asked the same open question: *does this
remind you of anything?* Do not prompt with candidate songs.

| Listener | Named an existing song? | What they named |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | yes / no | |
| 2 | yes / no | |
| 3 | yes / no | |

**Two or more listeners naming the same song is a fail regardless of total
score.** One naming something is a note to carry into revision. This gate sits
outside the 100 points — a demo cannot buy its way past it with hook recall.

## Deciding

Score all three before comparing. Choose on hook recall, vocal authenticity,
originality, edit discipline and paperwork — **not follower count, not
production polish, and not who was easiest to deal with.**

If the top two are within five points, the tiebreak is criterion 1, then the
similarity screen, then who answered the rights questions without hedging.
