# Chain of title — folder and gate checklist

**Internal.** Drives what counsel drafts, and what must exist before final
payment is released.

This is production guidance, not legal advice. Have Australian entertainment
counsel adapt an Arts Law or equivalent music commissioning template, and review
the final agreement.

---

## Why this is a folder and not a line in an invoice

Paying for a commission does not transfer the composition. Under the Australian
position, the composer or lyricist is generally the first owner, and the
commissioner needs an agreement to own it. The sound recording is a **separate
copyright**. Performers may hold their own interests on top of both.

So there are at least three things to acquire, from potentially eight or nine
people, and the only evidence that it happened is signed paper. That paper is
what this folder holds.

purpose-recorded room signs a release, and the murmur stem must contain no

## What the assignment must say

- **Present assignment**, not a promise to assign. All right, title and interest
  in the composition, lyrics, arrangement, master, performances and project
  files, to [OWNING ENTITY], worldwide, for the full copyright term including
  extensions, effective on payment.
- **Backup licence** — exclusive, irrevocable, transferable and sublicensable,
  operative if any part of the assignment is ineffective.
- **Permitted exploitation** — advertising, brand, film, social, web, broadcast,
  events, retail, games, remix, edit, translation, adaptation, AI-assisted post
  tools, platform distribution and future media, with no further approval or fee.
- **Moral rights** — specific, informed written consents for non-attribution,
  edits, excerpts, overlays and juxtaposition. Australian moral rights remain
  personal to the author; a blanket "waiver" is not the mechanism, and relying on
  one is the most common failure in a pack that otherwise looks complete.
- **Performer consent** — recording, fixation, editing, sync, communication,
  publicity, and assignment of any performer or master interest. Confirm no
  union or reuse payments unless stated.
- **Originality warranties** — no infringement, sample, interpolation, cloned
  voice, restricted loop, or conflicting publishing/label/management agreement.
  No generative output unless pre-approved and fully disclosed. Indemnity
  proportionate, supported by approval controls.
- **Payment** — fixed fee; milestones at 30% start / 30% approved demo / 40%
  delivery and signed chain of title; two revision rounds; kill fee; no
  royalties, points or backend.
- **PRO / publishing** — societies disclosed before signing. Address licence-back
  and opt-out mechanics if direct brand control of communication and performance
  rights is needed. **Assignment alone may not unwind a prior society mandate**,
  which is why society status is checked before the contract, not after.

## Release gate

Nothing ships and no final payment is released until all six are evidenced.

| Gate | Evidence required | ☐ |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | Signed assignments from every lyricist, composer and arranger; split schedule totalling 100%; society status checked. | ☐ |
| Master | Producer and all performers assign or consent; [OWNING ENTITY] named owner in metadata and delivery memo. | ☐ |
| Inputs | Zero uncleared samples; loop and software licences documented; crowd audio purpose-recorded and released. | ☐ |
| AI | Tool, account tier, date, prompt/input and a snapshot of the terms accepted **on that date** are recorded; counsel approves any retained output. | ☐ |
| Brand use | Specific moral-rights consents and broad edit/sync rights executed. | ☐ |
| Payment | Masters, sessions, stems, cutdowns and the complete signed rights pack all received. | ☐ |

The AI gate wants a snapshot of the terms as accepted on the day, not a link.
Generative-tool terms change, and a link resolves to whatever they say later.

---

## Delivery memo

One page, signed at handover, naming [OWNING ENTITY] as owner of both the
composition and the master, listing every delivered asset, and referencing the
contributor schedule. It is the document someone reads in three years when the
question is who owns this — and it is worth more than the folder it summarises.
