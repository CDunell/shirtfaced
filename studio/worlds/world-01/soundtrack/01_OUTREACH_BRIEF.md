# Commission brief — original brand anthem

**Send to:** 5–8 writer-producers (local network, AirGigs, SoundBetter).
**Do not book from a service description alone.**

---

## The assignment

Write and produce a fully original **2:34–2:48 euphoric Australian Friday-night
indie/pub anthem** at **132 BPM**, for a national streetwear brand's flagship
film and social campaign.

It must make a packed pub's collective release believable — without novelty pub
rock, artist imitation, samples, or reference to any existing song.

- **Working title:** "All In Tonight"
- **Signature crowd release:** "Hands up / hearts out / whole room / right now."
- **Lyrical thesis:** the week stays outside.

The song is not about drinking or larrikin caricature. It is about arriving
depleted and becoming electrically present because everyone else is present too.
The chorus has to be readable on first hearing and physically easy to shout.

---

## Fixed musical parameters

| | |
|---|---|
| Tempo / meter | 132 BPM, 4/4. Straight eighth-note drive, human push in choruses. No four-on-the-floor EDM feel. |
| Key / range | D major, verses coloured by B minor. Lead target A3–A4, optional D5 double at the final release. Transposition to C or E is open until casting. |
| Length | 2:34–2:48 |
| Hero section | A purpose-built 12.5-second section at approximately 1:02, which must also work from silence. Detail below. |

A suggested chorus progression, lyric, arrangement timeline and instrumentation
palette will be supplied with the full specification on shortlisting. The
composer is free to improve the harmony while preserving distinctiveness.

---

## The 12.5-second hero section

This is the centre of the commission. A dedicated section — not a chop out of
the chorus — so the film never depends on cutting a lyric mid-thought. It must
work cold from silence *and* connect naturally out of chorus 1.

| Clock | Audio event |
|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 s | Filtered room inhale plus bass pickup. No lyric yet. |
| 1.0–3.2 s | Lead: "Now we're all in tonight," over the harmonic lift. |
| 3.2–7.0 s | Four quarter-note gang attacks: "HANDS UP! / HEARTS OUT! / WHOLE ROOM! / RIGHT NOW!" |
| 7.0–10.7 s | Lead plus gang: "If the week wants a fight / it can wait outside." |
| 10.7–12.5 s | "All in tonight" tag; hard stop at 12.0, owned room tail to 12.5. |

Picture cuts to a distinct visual reaction on each of the four attacks, so those
four hits must be rhythmically unambiguous and land without rushing.

---

## Originality requirements — non-negotiable

- **No samples, interpolations, purchased beats, loop-library melodic phrases,
  uncleared crowd recordings, or cloned/synthesised voices.** Any production
  loops must be disclosed and licensed for standalone commercial release.
- **No "in the style of."** Do not work from a reference recording as a
  structural template, and do not name an artist, band, producer or song in any
  brief or tool prompt. Describe energy, density, texture and dramatic function
  instead.
- **No generative full-song output.** AI is acceptable for disposable ideation
  only, and any retained output must be declared with tool, account tier, date
  and input.
- The work will be **similarity-screened by a human panel** before approval —
  melody contour, hook rhythm, bass motif, progression and voicing, lyric phrase
  and arrangement silhouette. If listeners consistently name an existing song,
  it is revised before mastering.

## Rights — read before quoting

The commissioner requires **100% ownership of both the composition and the
master**, worldwide, in perpetuity, under a signed written assignment prepared
by Australian counsel.

- Every writer, producer, vocalist, musician and subcontractor assigns their
  interest and is disclosed by legal name. No undisclosed contributors.
- Performers sign consent covering recording, editing, sync, communication and
  publicity.
- Specific informed moral-rights consents are required for non-attribution,
  edits, excerpts and overlays.
- No royalties, points or backend. Fixed fee, paid against milestones.
- Credit is available in metadata where it is legally or operationally useful.
  Ownership and authorship are not the same thing, and the assignment does not
  ask you to pretend you did not write it.

"Royalty-free," a work-for-hire line in a service description, or a marketplace
default is **not sufficient**. If you are not willing to sign an assignment, say
so now — it costs us both less at this stage than at delivery.

## Deliverables the quote must cover

**Masters** — 24-bit/48 kHz WAV. Full master; full instrumental; TV mix (lead
down); no-gang; gang-only feature; a cappella. Unmastered premaster with at
least 3 dB true-peak headroom.

**Cutdowns** — 6 s, 10 s, 12.5 s, 15 s, 30 s and 60 s; hard-stop and tail
variants; one seamless 8-bar instrumental loop. Plus lead-lyric, gang-only,
instrumental and drums-plus-bass versions of the hero hook.

**Stems** — drums (kick, snare/clap, hats, toms, percussion, room, printed bus);
bass (DI, processed, bus); guitars (rhythm L/R, lead/answer, texture, bus); keys
and synth; vocals (lead dry, lead processed, doubles, harmonies, gang tight,
gang loose, gang room, crowd murmur as a separate stem); FX and room. All stems
start at 00:00 and are sample-aligned.

**Session and documentation** — native DAW session plus consolidated audio from
00:00, reconstructible without missing plug-ins. Tempo map, bar/beat markers,
chord chart, lyric sheet, cue sheet data, and a complete sample/loop/plug-in
disclosure log including "none used" declarations.

**Editability** — clean downbeats, pickups, cymbal-free transitions and at least
two hard endings recorded. Every campaign edit must cut without a cymbal tail or
a lyric fragment betraying the join.

**Mastering** — around -10 to -9 LUFS integrated as a starting range, true peak
no higher than -1 dBTP. Punch over loudness; the final call follows a platform
test, not a number.

## Process from here

1. You reply with the above.
2. We shortlist three and pay a credited test fee for a 45–60 second bespoke
   demo, under a short agreement that assigns the demo if selected.
3. One producer is chosen on hook recall, vocal authenticity, originality, edit
   discipline and paperwork.
4. Composition is approved at piano/guitar demo stage, before expensive
   production.
5. Full production, then a picture test of the 12.5-second section before
   mastering.
