# SHIRTFACED — MOMENTS

Every world as a list of single images. One line each. If it can't be described in
one line and seen with the sound off, it isn't a moment.

★ = carries a post on its own.

## What this is for

The unit of production is the moment, not the scene. A scene is where a moment
happens and what it costs. The feed only ever sees moments.

**Eighteen carry a post on their own across three worlds.** Everything marked ·
supports one, goes to Stories, or lives on the site as a still.

Two were cut for not being moments: glasses going onto a tray, and five people
waiting at a red light. The scenes went with them.
