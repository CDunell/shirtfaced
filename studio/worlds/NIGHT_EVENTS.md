# SHIRTFACED — NIGHT EVENTS

Source material. Not canon, not an arc, not a scene list.

Things that happen when a group of people get together with a few drinks in them.
Everything here is either something you have done or something you have watched
somebody do. Nothing invented.

Scenes get built from this. If a beat isn't on this list or the same species as
something on it, it's probably invented and it probably won't land.

## How to use it

One per scene. Two at most. A scene with three of these in it is a montage.

If a scene has none of them, it isn't a scene — it's texture, and texture goes in
the Story reservoir or on the website, never in one of ten feed slots.
