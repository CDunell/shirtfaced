# BORROWED CHAIRS

*World 02. One Saturday. Brock, Ethan, Grace, Mitch, SK, Charlotte, Sophie, Gary.*

*The readable cut. `STORY_ARC.md` is the production version.*

---

### 2:40pm — the backyard

A rented weatherboard two streets back from the flats. The lawn has been dead
since December.

One trestle table. An esky. Four chairs, and none of them match.

One of them is a dark timber pub stool with no back.

A tray-back ute is reversed up the side driveway with the tray down, which is
where six people will end up sitting by five o'clock.

Brock has organised all of this and has paid for almost none of it. He is on the
phone to somebody about a thing that was supposed to arrive at two.

Twelve people were asked.

---

### 3:04pm — the barbecue

Ethan lights it before anybody's asked anyone to.

He is nineteen months into being Grace's boyfriend and he still stands like it's
an interview.

Grace hands him the tongs and goes and sits down.

---

### 4:15pm — the gap in the fence

Sophie has gone next door.

Not to complain. To ask.

She comes back through the gap in the fence with four neighbours behind her and
every one of them is carrying a chair.

Twenty minutes later she has been two doors further down.

By five there are people in this yard who have never met Brock and have brought
their own esky.

Brock is delighted and has not counted anybody.

Sophie is not finished.

---

### 4:40pm — the barbecue

There are five blokes at the barbecue.

One of them is cooking.

The other four have their arms folded and all of them have a view.

Mitch is a chef. He is telling a twenty-year-old about heat.

Ethan hasn't said a word to any of them and hasn't taken his eyes off the plate.

Nobody leaves.

---

### 5:20pm — the side driveway

Gary is here.

He is leaning on the fence down the side of the house with a beer, talking to
somebody's father.

Nobody mentions it.

---

### 6:30pm — the gas

It runs out with about twenty people still waiting.

Nobody has a spare bottle.

Sophie goes back through the gap in the fence.

By ten past seven there is food going on three barbecues in three backyards and
two of them belong to people nobody here has met.

Ethan will not leave his.

He is cooking for forty people on the only one that doesn't work yet, waiting on a
bottle somebody's gone to get, and he will not come inside.

The string lights go on and there aren't enough of them. The yard goes blue for
about twenty minutes and nobody turns a light on in the house.

The shed is the bar now, because that's where the second esky ended up.

---

### 7:45pm — the corner

Charlotte finds the hire docket taped to the thing Brock hired.

She reads it.

There's a name on it and it isn't Brock's.

She puts it back down.

---

### 8:50pm — the table

More people keep arriving and there is more food than there was at four.

SK looks at the table for about four seconds, then moves everything on it.

She doesn't ask anybody and she doesn't tell anybody she's doing it.

By the time she walks away there is twice as much room and nobody has noticed
there was a problem.

Ethan is still at the barbecue. It's been going again since half seven.

Grace watches him from a chair that came from next door and doesn't get up.

---

### 10:30pm — the tongs

Gary walks over with two beers.

He hands one to Ethan and holds out his other hand.

Ethan gives him the tongs.

He wouldn't give them to a chef and he gives them to Gary without being asked
twice.

Nobody says anything about that.

He sits down for the first time since three o'clock.

Mitch, at some point, has started eating.

---

### 11:40pm — the yard

There are more chairs than people now.

Not one of them came from this house.

Brock is standing in the middle of his own party with an empty cup, telling
somebody's neighbour something at length.

The pub stool is under the string lights with nobody on it.

---

### Sunday, about nine

Four of them are standing in the middle of the yard holding chairs.

Nobody knows whose is whose.

Two have been carried back through the gap in the fence already and one of those
has come back.

The lawn is dead where the esky was.

Sophie is asleep in the last chair and nobody is going to be the one to ask her to
get up.
