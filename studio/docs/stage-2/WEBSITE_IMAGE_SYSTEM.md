# SHIRTFACED — WEBSITE IMAGE SYSTEM

**Status:** Locked foundation  
**Stage:** 2  
**Date:** 5 August 2026

## 1. Purpose

The website is the organised version of the memory. It must make the product easy to understand and buy without creating a visual break from Instagram or the documentary archive.

The journey is: **Instagram → homepage → collection → product → cart → checkout → delivery**. Commercial clarity increases through the journey; the world never changes companies.

## 2. Image roles

Every placement performs one role: world entry, collection introduction, product desire, product clarity, fit explanation, construction proof, environmental continuity, campaign progression, belonging, transitional pause, utility or post-purchase continuity.

Across the overall site, target roughly 55–65% world/lifestyle, 25–35% clear product and 10–15% detail/object/packaging. Page needs override the global ratio.

## 3. Homepage

The homepage answers: what does Shirtfaced feel like, what is available and where do I go next?

Recommended sequence:

1. Saveworthy documentary hero.
2. Restrained current-collection introduction.
3. Clearer product moment.
4. Small curated product group.
5. Documentary continuation from the same campaign period.
6. Fabric, construction or object detail.
7. Additional range.
8. Selected archive frames.
9. Quiet late-night or morning-after close.

Copy over the hero is limited to collection name, one short line and one action. No permanent discount language, cluttered badges or repeated calls to action.

## 4. Collection pages

A collection is a chapter, not a filtered grid with a banner. Alternate documentary introduction, manageable product groups, environmental product frames, movement and a quiet close. Track which garment, colour and cast member receives the first and largest treatment.

## 5. Product pages

The page converts emotional interest into informed confidence. It must show the correct variant, front, useful alternate or rear, fit, movement, fabric, construction and environmental belonging.

Gallery sequence follows `PRODUCT_PHOTOGRAPHY.md`: desire, clarity, fit, back/alternate, movement, construction, texture, object, documentary return. Each image adds different information.

Colour selection updates at minimum the primary, clarity, fit and colour-critical detail images. Do not recolour one source image into fake variants. Pair fit images with factual wearer height, size, intended fit and layering where available.

## 6. Campaign, archive and linking

Campaign pages may be immersive but always provide visible purchase paths. The archive holds complete photographic sequences and observational stories without immediate product pressure. Products that genuinely appear may be linked quietly with language such as “Worn here”; never overlay people with shop-the-look hotspots.

## 7. Cart through delivery

Cart imagery favours clear environmental or folded product frames. Checkout uses only product thumbnails and restrained brand treatment. Order confirmation and shipping may return gently to parcels on a bench, by a door or leaving in an ordinary vehicle. Never imply dispatch, manufacture or delivery facts that are not true.

## 8. Responsive behaviour

Mobile and desktop receive intentional crops. Preserve, in order, the emotional action, social relationship, product visibility, environmental identity and useful negative space. Product-clarity frames prioritise the correct product, garment shape, fit, body relationship and environmental evidence.

Store focal point, safe text area and minimum visible product area. Use alternate frames from the same moment when a master cannot survive another ratio.

## 9. World density and commercial clarity

| Rating | World density |
|---|---|
| W0 | Interface and products only |
| W1 | Trace of environment |
| W2 | Recognisable world; commerce leads |
| W3 | Story and commerce share the page |
| W4 | Immersive; world leads |

| Rating | Commercial clarity |
|---|---|
| C0 | No commercial path |
| C1 | Indirect discovery |
| C2 | Clear links exist |
| C3 | Products immediately understandable |
| C4 | Transactional completion |

Typical targets: homepage W4/C2–C3; collection W3/C3; product W2/C4; checkout W0–W1/C4.

## 10. Treatment, performance and accessibility

Preserve grain, shadow, skin and fabric texture. No universal overlays, fake borders, light leaks, retro presets or excessive sharpening. Video is observational, muted by default and lightly edited.

Use responsive sizes, modern formats, controlled compression, fixed dimensions and intentional crops. Photography never carries essential product information alone. Alternative text describes useful content plainly.

## 11. Studio records and review

Each placement records page, module, breakpoint, crop ratio, focal point, product, collection, prominence, scene, emotional role, commercial role, fallback and publication state. Track reuse, product/cast/camera rotation and variant accuracy.

Reject with codes such as `WEB_WORLD_DISCONNECT`, `WEB_TEMPLATE_FEEL`, `WEB_PRODUCT_UNCLEAR`, `WEB_GRID_MONOTONY`, `WEB_BAD_MOBILE_CROP`, `WEB_VARIANT_MISMATCH`, `WEB_GALLERY_DUPLICATE`, `WEB_FAKE_URGENCY`, `WEB_IMAGE_OVERUSE` and `WEB_ACCESSIBILITY_FAILURE`.

## 12. Launch minimum

Before launch: homepage hero and alternate; collection open and close; two documentary transitions; complete product galleries; fit and construction proof; environmental product frames; mobile and desktop crops; cart thumbnails; and relevant confirmation/shipping imagery.

The world and the store arrive together.
